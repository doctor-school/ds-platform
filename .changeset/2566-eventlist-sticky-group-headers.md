EventList: the group plates (days in «Будущие», months in «Прошедшие») stick to the top of the viewport while their group scrolls, as block behaviour — the desktop plate (label + rule) on the page-background surface, the mobile plate keeping the `DayBand` section background — hosts need no page CSS. New `stickyHeaderOffset` prop (number px or CSS length, default `0`) places them under a sticky storefront header.
