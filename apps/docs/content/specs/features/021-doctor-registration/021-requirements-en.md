# 021 — Doctor registration & consents (Requirements)

## Stage-A decisions in force

All three PRD forks are settled; nothing below re-opens them.

| Fork        | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **F-021-1** | **Б — the consent block is two-tier.** Owner pick 2026-08-25, verbatim «Б — два уровня (Рекомендуется)». The access conditions — the medical-worker declaration and the partner-data consent — are framed together above the submit button; the marketing opt-in stands separately below it. The canvas `consentVariant` default **А** (a flat checkbox list) is superseded; variants А and В are not built.    |
| **F-021-2** | **Б — the return context lives in the left half of the split, WITHOUT any back-navigation control on the card.** Owner pick 2026-08-25, verbatim (RU): «Вариант Б, но на карточке не должно быть кнопки, которая уводит назад - это странный UX». The canvas `returnVariant` default **А** (a line above the form) is superseded. At 390 the same card renders as the canvas's background plate above the form. |
| **F-021-3** | **Points are promised on the form**, not only on the success state. Owner pick 2026-08-25, verbatim (RU): «Обещание на форме». The canvas `promisePoints` default (off — success state only) is superseded. The value is settled: **+20 Pul for registration**, with **+30 Pul for profile completion** named as what completing the profile adds (REQ-49, owner 2026-08-25).                                   |

The canvas **state** props are not forks — they are content-driven obligations every 021 surface must handle: arrived **from a gate** vs **directly** (`fromGate`), arrived **on a partner link** vs **on their own** (`partnerLink`), and the form states empty / filled-and-valid / field errors / network failure (`formState`), plus the «letter sent» and «email confirmed» states the base canvas already carries. The canvas's composition switcher is a design-review aid and is not built.

## Outcomes

- A doctor stopped by a content gate registers where they were stopped, in a form short enough not to lose them: an email, a password, optionally a promo code — and no document of any kind at the door.
- The screen holds the doctor's intent visibly: the lesson, эфир or ticket they came for stands in the left half of the split (F-021-2), and after confirming the email they land back on it — the account page is never the default outcome of registering.
- The conditions of access and the marketing offer are told apart at a glance (F-021-1): the mandatory medical-worker declaration and the mandatory partner-data consent are framed together above the button, the optional newsletter opt-in sits separately below, and registration completes without the latter.
- A doctor knows what they are agreeing to: the partner-data consent names its exact composition — name, specialty, city, place of work — and states that contact details are not shared; each consent is recorded separately by purpose, with its version and date.
- A doctor who arrived on a partner's promo code or a medical representative's personal link sees who brought them, on the screen, and the attribution is attached to the resulting account rather than tagged invisibly.
- The form promises **+20 Pul for registering** and the success state credits it, naming what completing the profile adds (**+30 Pul**) in terms of what it unlocks — certificates and НМО — rather than by nagging.
- Every dead end is removed: an already-registered doctor is offered sign-in instead of being told their email is taken, a failed submission keeps what was typed, and a disabled button always says why next to itself.
- 021 re-specifies **no part of authentication**. Credentials, email confirmation, codes, rate limits and enumeration resistance stay with feature **003**, live in production; 021 builds the doctor storefront's surface over that engine and extends only its consent-purpose set and its post-registration cascade.

## Scope

**In:**

- The **registration route** `#d-register` in `apps/doctor` on `doctor.school`, rendered from the vendored `auth` canvas's split-screen composition inside 017's shell, `access: public`.
- The **form itself** — email, password, optional promo code — with each field's client-side validation rule and input mask declared, and no document upload, СНИЛС, diploma or file field anywhere on the surface.
- The **return context** in the left half of the split per F-021-2, rendering the content the doctor is registering for through the shared event-card unit owned by 018, carrying **no** back-navigation control, and absent — not empty — on a direct arrival.
- The **two-tier consent block** per F-021-1: the mandatory medical-worker declaration and the mandatory partner-data consent framed above the submit button, the optional marketing opt-in below it.
- The **consent record** — one row per purpose with its version and date, written through the existing per-purpose consent mechanics (003 EARS-20 / ADR-0009) — and the interface statement that change or withdrawal is a manager-handled request, with **no** self-service toggle rendered.
- The **attribution** of a promo code or a medical representative's personal link: visible on the screen in plain words and attached to the account created.
- The **points promise on the form** and the **credit on the success state** per F-021-3, with the profile-completion increment named as what it unlocks.
- The **post-confirmation landing**: the point of interest as the primary action, «в личный кабинет» as the secondary one, and a defined destination for a doctor with nothing to return to.
- The **enumeration-safe already-registered path** — the co-equal «Войти» / «Восстановить пароль» affordances of 003's existence-agnostic verification screen, rendered on the doctor storefront.
- **Actionable error states** in the field where they occurred, a disabled submit button that always states its reason beside it, and a failed submission that preserves what was typed.
- The **mailing-service handoff signal** for a doctor who opted in — the consent and the segmentation attributes 021 records; the connector itself is feature 042.
- Mobile-breakpoint parity at 390, both themes, and the `playwright-axe` accessibility bar on the registration route and its confirmation states.

**Out:**

- **Authentication itself** — the IdP, credential storage, password policy, the email verification code and its delivery, login, login-by-code, password reset, session issuance, the auth rate limits, bot protection and enumeration resistance are feature **003**, live in production. 021 supersedes no 003 clause; where the two touch, 021 references (see _Boundary with feature 003_).
- **Event registration proper** — what happens once the doctor is back on the эфир is feature **005**.
- **The doctor's account** — profile completion, document upload, verification statuses, the points balance display: feature **022**, with consent and document storage in **037**.
- **The points engine** — accrual rules, the append-only ledger, spending and the advance mechanics belong to feature **025**. 021 requests a registration accrual and renders a promise; it owns no ledger.
- **PRD US-14 — the points barrier in front of an эфир — is deferred out of 021.** «Not enough points before an event a doctor intends to attend» happens on the event page (**020**) against the accrual engine (**025**); the advance-availability flag the owner fixed is a property of an event, not of the registration form, and 021 renders no barrier, no advance affordance and no placeholder for one. Carried forward in the open questions below and flagged for its own tracked Issue.
- **The mailing integration** — the connector feeding the external service is feature **042**; 021 owns only the consent and the attributes that trigger it.
- **The partner cabinet and per-representative reporting** — feature **035**; 021 attaches the attribution, it does not report on it.
- **Document verification** and everything OWD-12 leaves open — not asked at the door, not decided here.
- **Any Academy-side registration or account-type choice** — one account, one sign-up (REQ-95); roles are attributes of one account (ADR-0016 §1).
- **The legal marking of partner material** — CON-8 / CON-14 assign the marking obligation to features **040** / **024**. 021 renders no marking and models no marking field.
- **Analytics on funnel conversion** and any A/B machinery for this form.

## Constraints

- **017 and 020 must land first.** The surface renders inside 017's shell and its return context needs 020's event page to return to; no 021 clause may be satisfied by re-implementing a shell or an event page, and none by building inside `apps/promo` or `apps/portal`.
- **No new authentication mechanism.** No social login, no magic link, no password policy of 021's own, no second consent model, no second verification code path. Every credential, code and session operation is a call into the contract feature 003 already runs.
- **Email is the only registration identifier here.** Phone is not a registration channel (003 EARS-2); the phone validation the `auth` canvas draws belongs to the sign-in path and is not built on this route (LD-9).
- **No documents at the door.** No upload control, no file input, no document field and no «прикрепите диплом» copy exists in any state of this surface (REQ-22).
- **No financing statement, anywhere.** No rendered string on this surface names who pays for the doctor's education; the only permitted statement is that it is free for the doctor. Cost, where it appears at all in the return context, is expressed in Pul, with zero cost reading «бесплатно для врача».
- **Glossary canon.** Where an organisation behind the platform is named it is «инвестор (организация)» / «первоинвестор»; «партнёр» is never used as the money-carrier in a doctor-facing string. The attribution line names the organisation or the representative as the source of the arrival, never as the payer.
- **The mandatory consent is mandatory, and withdrawal is not self-service.** The interface renders no withdrawal toggle and no «отозвать согласие» control; it states that a change or withdrawal is a request handled by a platform manager (REQ-34).
- **Enumeration resistance is not weakened by product copy.** No state of this surface may reveal whether an email is already registered — the already-registered doctor is served by co-equal sign-in affordances, never by a field error naming the fact (LD-2).
- **Honest states, no placeholders.** Every block renders exactly one of content, a skeleton, an honest statement, or an explicit Russian-language error with a working retry. An empty labelled box, a dead control, a silently disabled button and a «скоро» stub each fail review.
- **Design-system only.** Every element comes from `@ds/design-system` primitives with tokens-only styling and full interaction states; the vendored `auth` canvas is the composition source, and the split geometry, the field / focus / validation states and the «letter sent» / «email confirmed» states are taken from it unchanged.

## Prior decisions

- **ADR-0013 + AGENTS.md §6:** implementation runs the design-system-first gate and builds from the vendored `design-source/auth.dc.html` rather than from issue prose.
- **ADR-0006 §4:** the feature-spec triplet and flat EARS numbering; `it('EARS-N: …')` test titles.
- **Feature 017** owns the shell and the chosen specialty. **Feature 018** owns the event-card unit the return context renders. **Feature 020** owns the event page returned to. **Feature 003** owns authentication. 021 composes them and re-models none.

## Lead technical decisions

Each records a call the PRD left open. They are lead decisions in the AGENTS.md §6 decision-debt sense — reversible behind a stated contract, and named here rather than buried in the design.

- **LD-1 — 021 is a surface over 003's engine, and the seam is the request payload.** The doctor-storefront form issues the same registration command 003 owns, extended with the consent-purpose set, the attribution and the return target. Nothing about credentials, codes, sessions, limits or enumeration behaviour is re-implemented, re-configured or forked here; a 021 clause that could only be satisfied by changing an authentication mechanism is a defect, not a requirement.
- **LD-2 — the already-registered doctor is served by co-equal sign-in affordances, never by an «email taken» error.** PRD US-17 asks that the screen recognise the case; 003 EARS-16 forbids the response from disclosing it. Both are honoured by 003's existence-agnostic verification screen (EARS-24) rendered on this storefront: the doctor always reaches «письмо отправлено», where entering the code and «Войти» / «Восстановить пароль» stand as equal paths, and the account owner's private confirmation travels in the EARS-23 notice email. No field-level error, response body, status or timing distinguishes the two cases.
- **LD-3 — the return target is carried explicitly and resolved server-side; there is no «last page» heuristic.** The gate that sent the doctor here passes the target as an opaque, server-validated parameter which survives into the verification email's confirmation link and is resolved back to a route at confirmation time. No session-stored breadcrumb, no referrer sniffing, no client-held memory. This is also the contract 019 LD-7 depends on when it hands a card action into 021 with the feed URL attached.
- **LD-4 — a direct arrival lands on the doctor's specialty feed, falling back to the storefront home.** The PRD leaves the destination open between the feed (018), the home (017) and the account (022). The feed is the only one of the three that answers «what now» with content the doctor came to the platform for; the account is what the owner explicitly removed as a default outcome. When no specialty has been chosen yet, 017's shell resolves that choice first and the home is the fallback. The account is never the default landing, in either branch.
- **LD-5 — 021 declares three consent purposes on the existing versioned mechanism and owns none of the storage.** `medical-worker-declaration` and `partner-data-sharing` are mandatory preconditions of the command; `marketing-communications` is optional and absent when unticked. Each is written as its own versioned record with its date through 003 EARS-20 / ADR-0009, and lands in feature 037's store. A refusal is the absence of a record, never a record of refusal-as-consent, and 044's congress intake reuses these purposes rather than defining a second model.
- **LD-6 — the promised and credited point values are configuration read at render time, not literals in the form.** The owner fixed +20 Pul for registration and +30 Pul for profile completion; the surface renders whatever the configured values are, in the unit «Pul», and requests the accrual by emitting the registration event 025 consumes. 021 writes no ledger row itself, so changing the values is configuration rather than a spec change, and the promise on the form and the credit on the success state can never disagree.
- **LD-7 — the promo code is a real form field, and a link-borne attribution wins over a typed one.** The PRD leaves open whether a doctor ever types a code; the canvas draws the field and removing it would strand every offline campaign that prints a code. It is optional, validated server-side as an opaque token, and when the doctor also arrived on a medical representative's link the link's attribution is the one attached — a typed code never overwrites a resolved link.
- **LD-8 — a stale return target resolves to the nearest honest destination with a stated reason.** A doctor confirming the letter days later may return to content that has ended, filled up or been unpublished. Rather than a dead link or a silent redirect, the surface lands them on the closest live thing — the event's page when it still exists, the specialty feed otherwise — with a plain Russian line saying what happened to what they came for.
- **LD-9 — email only, and the code field is typed for the code that is actually sent.** Phone is not a registration identifier (003 EARS-2), so the canvas's phone validation is not built on this route. The verification-code field declares an `inputMode` that accepts the full alphanumeric code the IdP issues and applies **no** CSS uppercase transform, because the submitted value is verified case-sensitively — a numeric-only mobile keyboard or a display-layer uppercase both produce a code the doctor typed correctly and the system rejects.

## Event Model

### Commands

- `RegisterDoctor({ email, password, promoCode?, attribution?, consents: ConsentGrant[], returnTarget? })` — the 003 registration command (EARS-1) issued from this surface with the 021 payload. Rejects when either mandatory consent purpose is absent. Enumeration-resistant per 003 EARS-16.
- `ConfirmEmail({ code, returnTarget })` — 003's verification (EARS-3) with the return target of LD-3 carried through to the landing decision.
- `ResendVerificationCode({ identifier })` — 003 EARS-25, unchanged; rendered on this storefront's verification state.

### Events

021 emits:

| Emitted event                                                      | Consumer                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `ConsentGranted { purpose, version, grantedAt }` (one per purpose) | Feature **037** (storage, lifecycle), feature **042** (the marketing purpose only)                                              |
| `RegistrationAttributed { accountId, attribution }`                | Feature **035** (partner cabinet reporting)                                                                                     |
| `DoctorRegistered { accountId }`                                   | Feature **025** (the registration accrual of LD-6), feature **019** («вы записаны» / «Мои события» once a registration follows) |

021 consumes:

| Consumed event               | Effect on the screen                                                                                   |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `SpecialtyChosen` (017)      | Resolves the direct-arrival landing of LD-4.                                                           |
| `EmailVerified` (003 EARS-3) | Moves the surface to the success state: the credit, the profile-completion line and the return action. |
| `PointsCredited` (025)       | The success state states the credited amount as a fact rather than a promise.                          |

### Read models

- `RegistrationScreen { returnContext: ReturnContext | null, attribution: Attribution | null, pointsPromise: { registration: number, profileCompletion: number, unit: 'Pul' }, consentTiers: ConsentTier[], formState: 'empty' | 'valid' | 'errors' | 'network-failure' }` — the whole screen as a function of the entry URL.
- `ReturnContext { kind: 'event' | 'lesson' | 'ticket', card: PublicEventSummary, target: OpaqueReturnTarget }` — `null` on a direct arrival, rendered through 018's card unit with no back-navigation control (F-021-2).
- `Attribution { kind: 'promo-code' | 'representative-link', displayName, organisation }` — `null` when the doctor arrived on their own; the display names the source of the arrival and never a payer.
- `ConsentTier { tier: 'access-conditions' | 'marketing', items: ConsentItem[] }` — exactly two tiers per F-021-1.
- `ConsentItem { purpose, required: boolean, statement, dataComposition?: string[], excluded?: string[] }` — `partner-data-sharing` carries `dataComposition` (name, specialty, city, place of work) and `excluded` (contact details).
- `SuccessState { credited: { amount, unit }, profileCompletion: { amount, unit, unlocks: string[] }, primaryAction: ReturnTarget, secondaryAction: CabinetHref }` — the post-confirmation render.
- `FieldSpec { name, rule, mask, hint, errorSlot }` — the per-field validation contract of EARS-11.

### Policies

- The registration route is public; nothing on it is gated, and no payload is delivered to the client and hidden there.
- Both access-condition consents are preconditions of the command: without either, the command is refused server-side and the submit control is disabled client-side with its reason stated (never one without the other).
- The marketing consent alters no outcome of registration other than the presence of its own record and the 042 handoff.
- No response, status or timing on this surface differs between an existing and a new email (003 EARS-16, LD-2).
- The rendered screen is a function of the entry URL plus the resolved attribution; no state is restored from a previous visit.
- The point values rendered and the point values credited come from one configuration source (LD-6).

## EARS requirements

> Flat numbering per ADR-0006 §4. Every clause realizes one or more PRD stories and is covered by `021-scenarios.feature`.

- **EARS-1** _(realizes: US-1, US-2, US-22)_ — When a visitor opens `#d-register` on `doctor.school`, the storefront shall render the registration surface as the vendored `auth` canvas's split-screen composition inside feature 017's shell, `access: public`, asking for **an email, a password and an optional promo code and nothing else** — no document upload, file input, diploma, СНИЛС or document-related copy shall exist in any state of the surface — built from `@ds/design-system` primitives with tokens-only styling and the canvas's field, focus and validation states, defining no header, navigation or footer of its own and holding no private copy of a form primitive.
- **EARS-2** _(realizes: US-3, US-22)_ — When a doctor arrives from a content gate (`fromGate`), the surface shall render the **return context in the left half of the split** per F-021-2, showing the lesson, эфир or ticket they came for through the **shared event-card unit feature 018 owns** with no screen-local re-implementation, shall render **no back-navigation control on that card** (the owner's explicit condition on the pick), shall state plainly what the doctor will return to, and shall render the same card as the canvas's background plate above the form below the mobile breakpoint.
- **EARS-3** _(realizes: US-5)_ — When a visitor opens the registration route with **no return context**, the left half's context element shall be **absent from the tree rather than rendered empty**, the form shall remain fully usable, and registration shall complete to the defined destination of LD-4 — the doctor's specialty feed, falling back to the storefront home when no specialty has been chosen — and never to a dead return, an empty card frame or the account page as a default.
- **EARS-4** _(realizes: US-6)_ — The surface shall carry a **mandatory «Я являюсь медицинским работником» checkbox** as a precondition of registration, accompanied by a plain-language statement that confirmation is a legal requirement for part of the materials, with **no «ask later» form, no partial variant and no path that completes registration without it**; the checkbox shall be a **declaration, not verification** — no document is requested to support it, and content that requires confirmed medical-worker status stays gated on the verification specified in features 022 / 037.
- **EARS-5** _(realizes: US-7, US-8)_ — The consent block shall be rendered in **two tiers** per F-021-1: the **access conditions** — the medical-worker declaration and the **mandatory partner-data consent** — framed together **above** the submit button, and the **optional marketing opt-in** standing **separately below** it, the two tiers distinguishable by their rendering and not by wording alone; the partner-data consent shall state its **exact composition — name, specialty, city, place of work — and shall state that contact details are not shared**; no flat single-list rendering and no expandable-disclosure rendering of the data composition shall be built.
- **EARS-6** _(realizes: US-9)_ — The **marketing opt-in shall be genuinely optional**: registration shall complete with it unticked, its state shall alter no other outcome of the command, it shall never be pre-ticked, and it shall never be bundled into either access-condition consent.
- **EARS-7** _(realizes: US-10)_ — When a registration is accepted, the system shall record **each granted consent as its own versioned per-purpose record with its date** through the existing consent mechanism (003 EARS-20 / ADR-0009) — `medical-worker-declaration`, `partner-data-sharing` and, when granted, `marketing-communications` per LD-5 — an ungranted optional purpose producing **no record at all**; the interface shall state that a change or withdrawal is a **request handled by a platform manager** and shall render **no self-service withdrawal toggle or control** anywhere on this surface.
- **EARS-8** _(realizes: US-11, US-12)_ — When a doctor arrives on a medical representative's personal link or enters a promo code (`partnerLink`), the surface shall render the **attribution visibly on the screen** in plain words naming who brought them — never as a payer statement and never using «партнёр» as the money-carrier — and shall attach that attribution to the account created, a **link-borne attribution taking precedence over a typed code** per LD-7; a doctor who arrived on their own shall see the **same form with the attribution element absent**, not empty.
- **EARS-9** _(realizes: US-13)_ — The form shall **promise the registration points before submission** per F-021-3 («+N Pul за регистрацию», the unit rendered as «Pul»), and the post-confirmation success state shall state the **credited registration amount** and name **what completing the profile adds** and what it unlocks — certificates and НМО — with both the promise and the credit read from the **one configuration source of LD-6** so they can never disagree; 021 shall write no points ledger row itself, requesting the accrual from feature 025 by emitting `DoctorRegistered`.
- **EARS-10** _(realizes: US-4)_ — When the doctor confirms their email, the surface shall land them **back on the point of interest they came from** as the **primary** action, resolved server-side from the return target carried through the confirmation link per LD-3, with **«в личный кабинет» as a secondary action only**; the account page shall never be the default outcome of registration, no «last page» heuristic or client-held breadcrumb shall be used, and a **stale target** — content ended, filled or unpublished — shall resolve to the nearest honest destination with a plain statement of what happened per LD-8, never to a dead link or a silent redirect.
- **EARS-11** _(realizes: US-15, US-20)_ — **Every input on this surface shall declare its client-side validation rule and its input mask**, applied before submit with localized copy from the message catalog (003 EARS-21 / EARS-22, semantic field primitives per #197): **email** — shape validation, mask `none` (a mask would fight legitimate address forms); **password** — the single length-only rule «Не менее 8 символов» rendered as a persistent hint that stays visible **while** an error is shown, with the show-password toggle of 003 EARS-38, mask `none`; **promo code** — trimmed, length-bounded, otherwise opaque, mask `none` (the code vocabulary is a campaign's, not the form's); **verification code** — fixed-length, with an `inputMode` that admits the **full alphanumeric code** the IdP issues and **no CSS uppercase transform**, because the value is verified case-sensitively (LD-9). Client-side validation is a UX affordance only — the BFF and the IdP remain the credential authority.
- **EARS-12** _(realizes: US-15, US-16)_ — Every error shall be **actionable in the field where it occurred** — a malformed email, a too-short password, a rejected promo code — stating what to do rather than restating the obstacle, in Russian from the message catalog and programmatically tied to its field; and while a mandatory condition is unmet the **submit control shall be disabled with its reason stated beside it**, naming the specific unmet condition, so that a silently dead button exists in no state of this surface.
- **EARS-13** _(realizes: US-17)_ — When a registration targets an **already-registered** email, the surface shall behave **identically to the new-registrant case in body, status and timing** (003 EARS-16) and shall route the doctor to the **existence-agnostic verification state** rendered on this storefront, where entering the code and the **«Войти» / «Восстановить пароль» actions stand as co-equal affordances** per LD-2, the account owner's correct path additionally travelling in the 003 EARS-23 notice email; **no field error, response field, status or timing difference on this surface shall disclose that the email exists**, and no dead end «этот email уже занят» shall be rendered.
- **EARS-14** _(realizes: US-18)_ — When a submission fails on the network or with a server error, the surface shall **preserve everything the doctor typed** — every field value, every checkbox state and the resolved attribution — shall state the failure in Russian with a working retry that re-submits without re-filling, and shall keep the password within the volatile in-memory envelope 003 EARS-39 already defines, never writing it to the URL, storage or a cookie in order to survive the failure.
- **EARS-15** _(realizes: US-19)_ — Registration on this surface shall produce **one account that works on both storefronts** (REQ-95, ADR-0015 §4): the surface shall offer **no account-type choice** («врач / эксперт» — roles are attributes of one account per ADR-0016 §1), shall offer **no separate Academy sign-up**, shall link to none, and a doctor who already registered on either host shall never be asked to register again on the other.
- **EARS-16** _(realizes: US-20)_ — Every state of this surface — empty, filled-and-valid, field errors, network failure, «letter sent» and «email confirmed», with and without the return context, with and without an attribution — shall render in the canvas's mobile composition and stay fully operable **at 390 and at 1440 in both themes**, and the registration route and its confirmation states shall pass the `playwright-axe` gate with **every checkbox a real labelled control**, every error programmatically associated with its field, the **disabled-submit reason readable by a screen reader**, and the two consent tiers distinguishable to assistive technology rather than by visual framing alone.
- **EARS-17** _(realizes: US-21)_ — When a doctor grants the **marketing consent**, the system shall emit the `ConsentGranted { purpose: 'marketing-communications' }` record together with the doctor's **segmentation attributes**, which feature **042** consumes to add them to the external mailing base; a withdrawal recorded later shall propagate through the same record, and **021 shall implement no mailing transport, no list membership and no unsubscribe surface of its own** (REQ-115).
- **EARS-18** _(realizes: US-22 · process gate — not a code Issue)_ — Before implementation of the 021 surface begins, the team shall run the `build-ui-from-design-system` gate against **`design-source/auth.dc.html`** and the reused units (`webinar-card.dc.html` for the return context, the design system's field, checkbox and button primitives) as the composition source of truth, build with tokens-only styling and full interaction states, cover every `formState` × `fromGate` × `partnerLink` combination plus the «letter sent» and «email confirmed» states at both breakpoints and in both themes, and re-confirm the rendered result with the product owner on the live stand before merge; the recorded Stage-A picks (F-021-1 **Б**, F-021-2 **Б** without a back-navigation control, F-021-3 **promise on the form**) shall be treated as decisions rather than re-opened questions, and the canvas's composition switcher shall not be built.

## Invariants

- The surface renders inside exactly one shell — 017's — and defines no header, navigation or footer of its own.
- No document control of any kind exists in any state of this surface.
- Registration cannot complete without both access-condition consents, and completes freely without the marketing one.
- Exactly two consent tiers are rendered, and no self-service withdrawal control exists on this surface.
- Every granted consent has exactly one versioned per-purpose record with a date; an ungranted optional purpose has none.
- No response body, status or timing on this surface distinguishes an existing email from a new one.
- The return context card carries no back-navigation control, and is absent rather than empty when there is nothing to return to.
- The account page is never the default landing after registration.
- The points value promised on the form and the value credited on the success state come from the same configuration source.
- 021 writes no points ledger row, stores no consent itself, and implements no mailing transport.
- No rendered string, response field or copy on this surface states who finances the doctor's education, and «партнёр» never appears as the money-carrier.
- The submit control is never disabled without a stated reason beside it.
- No authentication mechanism of feature 003 is re-implemented, forked or reconfigured by this feature.

## Dependencies and sequencing

- **021 is blocked by 017 and 020.** The shell comes from 017; the destination the return context points at comes from 020. No 021 clause may be satisfied by re-implementing either.
- **021 depends on feature 003** for every authentication mechanism it calls, and supersedes none of it (see _Boundary with feature 003_).
- **EARS-1 and EARS-11 precede everything composed on top of them** — the form and its field contract are the substrate of EARS-4, EARS-5, EARS-12, EARS-13 and EARS-14.
- **EARS-10 depends on LD-3's return-target contract**, which is also what **019 LD-7** hands into this feature; the two must agree before either ships its guest path.
- **EARS-7 hands its records to feature 037** and **EARS-17 hands the marketing purpose to feature 042**; neither is implemented here, and 037 / 042 consume what 021 emits rather than the other way round.
- **EARS-9 depends on feature 025** for the accrual it requests; 021 renders the promise and the credited fact and writes no ledger row.
- **EARS-8's attribution is consumed by feature 035**; 021 attaches, 035 reports.
- **Features 022 and 044 are blocked by 021** — the cabinet is the state after registration, and the congress intake writes consents on 021's model rather than a second one.

## Open questions carried forward

These stay open; each is designed around above rather than resolved here, and each is a product decision the owner still owns.

- **The points barrier in front of an эфир (PRD US-14)** — deferred out of 021 per Scope → Out and flagged for its own tracked Issue. Whether a doctor short of Pul is admitted on advance is a property of an event and of the accrual engine (**025**) rendered on the event page (**020**); the owner has fixed the advance as a per-event configurable flag, and where that barrier is rendered and what it says are decisions those features own. 021 ships no barrier, no advance affordance and no placeholder.
- **Where a direct registration should land** — LD-4 chooses the specialty feed with a home fallback; the owner may prefer the home outright. Reversing it is changing a destination, not a mechanism.
- **How long a return context should survive** — LD-8 fixes the behaviour (nearest honest destination with a stated reason), not the window. Whether a target expires after a day, a week or never is an owner call the live funnel will inform.
- **Whether a doctor ever types a promo code by hand** — LD-7 ships the field the canvas draws; if the field proves unused, removing it is deleting a field, not unwinding attribution.
- **What refusing the mandatory consent shows in the funnel** — EARS-4 and EARS-5 guarantee only that registration cannot complete without it and that the reason is stated beside the disabled control; whether a declining doctor is shown anything further (an explanation page, a manager contact) is unresolved, and no placeholder for it ships.
- **Whether a secondary identifier is ever offered at registration** — LD-9 fixes email-only here, matching 003 EARS-2; a later phone-as-secondary surface is a 003 increment, not a 021 change.
