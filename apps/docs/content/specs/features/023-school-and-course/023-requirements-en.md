# 023 — The school and the course (Requirements)

## Outcomes

- A doctor chooses a **school** as they would choose a teacher: one screen shows who leads it, its courses as cards leading to the course path, what the courses lead to, what graduates say, and what the school gives beyond lessons.
- The **course page** is the 024 path with a header that says, in the doctor's words, what the course leads to and what it takes — never the name of a rules preset, never «модуль 1 из 1».
- What a finished module gives is a **module setting** (owner D18): points, a document, or both; the course certificate is always for the whole course.
- A school with no published course is visible as **«скоро»** and lets a doctor ask to see its courses in their feed; no date is fixed copy.
- Staff build the structure — schools, courses, modules, their order, clubs and rewards — and moderate reviews in `apps/admin` without a release.

## Scope

**In:**

- The **school's descriptive data** over the 024 school row (EARS-1 of 024): description, the «О школе» facts, the «скоро» note, the community link, the mentoring and internship offers; the **course's descriptive data** over the 024 course row: description, academic hours, the document it leads to, an optional partner organisation; the **module's club event** reference.
- The **module reward** setting `module.reward` (D18) in the 024 settings schema.
- The **doctor screens** on `apps/doctor`: the school screen `/schools/{schoolSlug}` per the approved canvas, the course page header on the 024 route `/courses/{courseSlug}`, the single-module presentation of the 024 path, reviews, mentoring requests and the interest capture.
- The **admin screens** in `apps/admin`: the school, course and module structure editor (order, module size, club, club in completion, reward), review moderation and the mentoring-request list.

### Out of scope

- **The course path, lessons, steps, the rules constructor, presets, facts and the evaluator** — feature **024**; 023 renders that path as the course page and reads the evaluator's statuses.
- **The school, course and module tables themselves** — 024 EARS-1 owns them; 023 adds columns and its own tables only.
- **Points** — feature **025**; 023 decides only whether a module reports `module.completed` (EARS-14).
- **Issuing a document** (certificate, НМО, a module document) — feature **038**; **document verification** upload and statuses — feature **037**. 023 reads their statuses and requests issuance through their contracts; slices that need them are `blocked_by` those features.
- **The mentoring or internship application process** after the request (EARS-16) — a separate feature when specified.
- **A list of a direction's schools** (US-1) beyond the school screen: no approved canvas exists; the school screen is reached from the 018 feed and the 017 specialty page links. A new list surface needs the owner's Stage A first — tracked by the lead as a follow-up Issue at `open-ears-issues`.
- **The Academy's backstage** (projects, feature 030) and the **game layer** of the mobile app.

## Constraints

- **024 is the model.** No second structure, rule, preset or status: school and course screens read the 024 rows, the resolved settings and the evaluator's statuses (024 EARS-5, EARS-11); progress is never a stored percentage.
- **Copy from data.** Counts, hours, dates, the «скоро» note and every rule sentence come from data and the 024 sentence builder (024 EARS-17); no doctor text says «бесплатно», speaks of money or who pays, says «проект», «сериал» or «N серий», or names a preset.
- **One package.** `packages/learning` owns the school and course blocks (`./ui`) and the editor blocks (`./admin`); `apps/doctor` adds only `app/schools/[schoolSlug]/page.tsx` and host-config fields, `apps/admin` only route files (ADR-0013 §A1). Course cards reuse the 018 doctor content card `FeedCard` (`packages/design-system/src/blocks/feed-card.tsx`; the canvas's `unit-content-card`), club events the `unit-event-card` of the doctor storefront.
- **No purchase.** No school, course or module carries a price for the doctor (D10).

## Prior decisions

- **ADR-0016 §3:** the leading expert is a person with the expert role. **§4:** school, course and module carry a project reference; the doctor never sees it. **§5:** a school hangs on a direction, several per direction, one per expert; a course always lives in a school. **§7:** document records are 037's and 038's. **§8:** 023's doctor rows are `doctor`; moderation state and editor rows `admin-only`.
- **ADR-0013 §A1:** `packages/learning`, the same capability as 024. **ADR-0014:** this triplet formalises `023-product.md`.
- **024:** the rows (EARS-1), the layers and presets (EARS-5, EARS-9), the evaluator (EARS-11), the 025 emission (EARS-12), the course path block (EARS-14), the sentence builder (EARS-17) and the admin access envelope (EARS-30, EARS-31).
- **Owner D18:** module reward = points | document | both, default points; the course certificate stays.
- **Lead, 2026-11-12:** «скоро» with interest capture and data-driven date text; graduate-only, moderated reviews; club not in the module completion by default (024 EARS-6, `course.clubInCompletion`); the preset's name never shown; a single-module course hides its module level.
- **Owner Stage A, 2026-11-12:** the school canvas and its forks (cards, «Вы учитесь» + rows over six, document block after courses, verification band).

## Event Model

**Commands** — `UpdateSchoolDetails`, `PublishSchool`, `UnpublishSchool`, `UpdateCourseDetails`, `ReorderCourses`, `PublishCourse`, `UnpublishCourse`, `ReorderModules`, `SetModuleClub`, `SetModuleReward` (admin, the reward and club-in-completion through 024's `SetRuleLayer`); `ModerateReview { publish | reject }` (admin); `SubmitReview`, `RequestMentoring`, `RegisterSchoolInterest` (doctor).

**Events** — `SchoolPublished`, `CoursePublished`, `ReviewSubmitted`, `ReviewPublished`, `ReviewRejected`, `MentoringRequested`, `SchoolInterestRegistered` (each change a 010 audit row for staff actions); consumed: 024 `ModuleCompleted`, `CourseCompleted`; produced to 038: `module-document.requested`, `course-certificate.requested`.

**Read models** — the school (header, facts, course cards with per-doctor states, document block, club events, mentoring, reviews summary); the course header; the admin school / course / module editor; the review moderation queue; the mentoring-request list.

**Policies** — «a school screen shows only what 024 computes»; «a preset's name never reaches a doctor»; «a review is visible only after moderation, and only a graduate may write one»; «a module reward decides only whether 025 hears the module and whether 038 is asked for a module document»; «the course certificate is always for the whole course».

## EARS requirements

> Flat numbering per ADR-0006 §4. Every clause realizes one or more PRD stories and is covered by `023-scenarios.feature`.

- **EARS-1** _(realizes: US-2, US-3, US-8, US-14)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL keep, over the 024 school row, the school's description, up to four «О школе» facts (a label and a text each), an optional «скоро» note, an optional community link and optional mentoring and internship offer texts; over the 024 course row, its description, academic hours, the document it leads to (`certificate` | `certificate-nmo`) with its prerequisites text, an optional partner organisation of the 032 registry and its position in the school; over the 024 module row, an optional club event of 020; every such column SHALL be `doctor` (ADR-0016 §8) and no new school, course or module table SHALL exist.
- **EARS-2** _(realizes: US-14, US-10)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL add the setting `module.reward` with the values `points`, `document` and `both`, platform default `points`, settable at the course and the module layers and resolved by 024 EARS-5 like every other setting; no value of it SHALL change the course's completion or its certificate.
- **EARS-3** _(realizes: US-2, US-6, US-13, US-15)_ — WHEN any caller reads `GET /v1/learning/schools/{schoolSlug}`, THE SYSTEM SHALL answer the published school with its direction, its leading expert, the data of EARS-1, its status `open` or — WHILE it has no published course — `soon`, the numbers of its published courses and of their published lessons, and its published courses in school order, each with its title, description, lesson count, academic hours, document, partner and, for a signed-in doctor, its state `not-started` | `started` | `done` computed by the 024 evaluator; for a signed-in doctor it SHALL also carry the 037 verification status of the doctor's documents; an unknown or unpublished school SHALL be answered `404`.
- **EARS-4** _(realizes: US-2)_ — WHEN a doctor or a guest opens `doctor.school/schools/{schoolSlug}`, THE SYSTEM SHALL render the school block of `packages/learning` per `design-source/doctor-school.dc.html`: the poster header with the breadcrumb of the direction's specialty and the school, the school's title and description and the counts «N курсов · M уроков» built from EARS-3, the «О школе» facts, and the sections of EARS-5…EARS-20 in the canvas order; WHILE the school loads it SHALL show the canvas loading state; an unknown school SHALL render the storefront's not-found page.
- **EARS-5** _(realizes: US-2, US-3, US-6)_ — WHILE the school has at most six published courses, THE SYSTEM SHALL show each as the 018 content card `FeedCard` (`packages/design-system/src/blocks/feed-card.tsx`; the canvas's `unit-content-card`) in a vertical list in school order with its lesson count, academic hours, the document it leads to and its EARS-3 state, each card leading to `/courses/{courseSlug}`; WHILE it has more than six, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the courses the signed-in doctor has started or done as cards under «Вы учитесь» and the rest as rows, with «Найти курс» filtering by title, «Показать ещё 10» revealing the next ten rows, and a no-match message naming the query.
- **EARS-6** _(realizes: US-2, US-1)_ — WHILE the school's status is `soon`, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the badge «Скоро» in the header, the courses section state «Школа готовится» with the school's «скоро» note when set and the interest capture of EARS-7, and the empty states of the events and reviews sections; no expected date or season SHALL be fixed copy.
- **EARS-7** _(realizes: US-1, US-13)_ — WHEN a signed-in doctor uses the interest capture of a `soon` school, THE SYSTEM SHALL add the specialty of the school's direction to the doctor's specialties through the 017 specialty contract, idempotently, and confirm that the school's courses will appear in the doctor's 018 feed; WHEN a guest uses it, THE SYSTEM SHALL lead to registration (021) with the school screen as the return target and complete the capture on return.
- **EARS-8** _(realizes: US-13)_ — WHILE the viewer is a guest, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the school, its courses and their paths readable in full and, over the courses, the gate that starting a lesson needs registration with a return to the same place (021); no gated step content SHALL be served (024 EARS-22).
- **EARS-9** _(realizes: US-10)_ — WHILE a signed-in doctor's documents are not confirmed by 037 and the school has a published course, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the verification band over the courses saying that the courses are open without documents and that the certificate and НМО need confirmed documents, with the entry to the 037 document upload in the doctor's account; the same entry SHALL be reachable from every course page at any time.
- **EARS-10** _(realizes: US-10, US-5)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL show after the courses the document block: what the school's courses lead to, from each course's document of EARS-1 (the certificate for the whole course, НМО where the document is `certificate-nmo`), and, for a signed-in doctor whose course is done by the 024 evaluator, «Скачать сертификат» with the 038 document WHILE 038 has issued it, or the statement that the certificate waits for document verification with the entry of EARS-9 WHILE 037 has not confirmed the documents.
- **EARS-11** _(realizes: US-3, US-10)_ — WHEN a doctor or a guest opens `doctor.school/courses/{courseSlug}`, THE SYSTEM SHALL render above the 024 path block (024 EARS-14) the course header: the breadcrumb of the school, the title, the description, the leading expert, the academic hours and the lesson count, the document the course leads to with its prerequisites, the rules summary built by the 024 sentence builder from the resolved rules — what opens when and what counts as done — and the document entry of EARS-9; the header SHALL never show a preset's name.
- **EARS-12** _(realizes: US-11)_ — WHILE a course has exactly one module, THE SYSTEM SHALL render its path and the «Модули и уроки» outline without the module level — no module section header, no module number, the outline listing the lessons directly — and no doctor screen SHALL say «модуль 1 из 1».
- **EARS-13** _(realizes: US-5)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL state on the course header and the document node that the certificate needs the whole course, built from the course's resolved completion rule (024 EARS-6, EARS-17); WHILE the course's rules open every lesson immediately (the «Открытый курс» preset of 024 EARS-9 or an equivalent override), a doctor SHALL open any lesson directly and the earlier lessons SHALL stay not done on the path.
- **EARS-14** _(realizes: US-10, US-14)_ — WHEN a doctor's module status first turns `done` (024 EARS-12), THE SYSTEM SHALL — WHILE the module's resolved `module.reward` includes points — let 024 record `module.completed` to 025, and — WHILE it includes a document — request the module document from 038 with the person, the module and the instant, its request id derived from the person and the module so a re-completion is a duplicate; WHEN a doctor's course first turns `done`, THE SYSTEM SHALL request the course certificate from 038 the same way, whatever the modules' rewards.
- **EARS-15** _(realizes: US-2, US-8)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL show the school's events section as the `unit-event-card` list of the scheduled 020 events that are the clubs of the school's published modules (EARS-1), nearest first, at most four, with «Все события →» leading to the storefront's events page; WHILE there is none, the section SHALL say that the school's events will appear with its courses.
- **EARS-16** _(realizes: US-8)_ — WHILE the school has a mentoring or internship offer, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the mentoring section with the offers' texts and «Хочу к наставнику», enabled only WHILE the signed-in doctor has done a course of the school and otherwise replaced by the statement that mentoring opens after the whole course; WHEN the doctor sends the request, THE SYSTEM SHALL store one open mentoring request per doctor and school with the instant, confirm it on the screen, list it to staff on the school editor screen (EARS-26), and refuse a doctor without a done course with `403 MENTORING_NOT_ELIGIBLE`.
- **EARS-17** _(realizes: US-2)_ — WHILE the school has a community link, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the community section with its title and the link; otherwise the section SHALL be absent.
- **EARS-18** _(realizes: US-7)_ — WHEN a signed-in doctor who has done a course of the school by the 024 evaluator submits a review of that course — one to five stars and a text —, THE SYSTEM SHALL store it as `pending` with the person, the course and the instant, keep one review per doctor and course (a later one replacing it and returning to `pending`), and refuse a doctor without that done course with `403 REVIEW_NOT_GRADUATE` and nothing stored.
- **EARS-19** _(realizes: US-7)_ — WHEN a staff member publishes or rejects a pending review on the moderation screen in `apps/admin`, THE SYSTEM SHALL change its state, leave one 010 change-audit row, and show only `published` reviews on any doctor read.
- **EARS-20** _(realizes: US-7)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL show the reviews section with the average stars and the count of the school's published reviews, the latest published reviews with each author's specialty and city from the doctor profile and never the name, and «Все отзывы →» revealing the rest; WHILE there is none, the empty state saying a review can be left after the course and, for a graduate, the review form of EARS-18; WHEN the reviews read fails, an alert with «Повторить» while the rest of the school screen keeps working.
- **EARS-21** _(realizes: US-12)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL carry in every lesson read (024 `GET /v1/learning/lessons/{lessonId}`) the course slug and the lesson's first step, so any lesson link — the 018 «урок дня» included — opens the 024 step route inside its course, and the step screen SHALL show the course title linking back to the course path with that lesson in view.
- **EARS-22** _(realizes: US-9)_ — WHILE a course carries a partner organisation, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the 024 partner mark with the partner's name from the 032 registry on its card and its header; no other partner or advertising content SHALL appear on the school or course screens.
- **EARS-23** _(realizes: US-2, US-3, US-13)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL build every count, duration, date and promise on the school and course screens from data, and no text of these screens SHALL say «бесплатно» in any form, speak of a price, money or who pays, say «проект», «сериал» or «N серий», or name a rules preset.
- **EARS-24** _(realizes: US-2)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL serve the school screen only on the doctor storefront: the Academy host SHALL have no `/schools/{schoolSlug}` route and SHALL answer it with its not-found page.
- **EARS-25** _(realizes: US-15)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL serve the school read, reviews and mentoring requests through REST endpoints with Zod contracts in `packages/schemas` to the website and the mobile app, so a doctor's course states are the same on both; WHILE the viewport is a phone, the school screen and the course header SHALL work without horizontal page scroll with every control keyboard-reachable, and each SHALL pass the platform's `playwright-axe` gate.
- **EARS-26** _(realizes: US-14)_ — WHEN a staff member opens «Школы» in `apps/admin`, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the school editor built from `@ds/design-system`: the list of schools by direction, create a school (direction, leading expert, title, slug, project) through the 024 admin API, edit the data of EARS-1, publish and unpublish the school, and the school's mentoring requests; creating a second school for the same expert and direction SHALL be refused with a field error.
- **EARS-27** _(realizes: US-14, US-11)_ — WHEN a staff member opens a school's courses in `apps/admin`, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the course and module editor built from `@ds/design-system`: create a course in the school, order the courses, edit the course data of EARS-1, add, rename and order modules, set each module's club on or off with its 020 event, and set the course's module size, club in completion and the course's and each module's `module.reward` as 024 rule settings with their «Унаследовано от» source; it SHALL show a module's lesson count next to the module size as information and never refuse it; WHEN the staff member publishes a course without a module holding a published lesson, THE SYSTEM SHALL refuse with `409 PUBLISH_REQUIREMENTS_NOT_MET` naming it.
- **EARS-28** _(realizes: US-14, US-7)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL serve every 023 admin endpoint under the access envelope of 024 EARS-30 — the dedicated admin session, CSRF, a canonical idempotency key, `If-Match` with `412 PRECONDITION_FAILED` on a stale version — to staff granted content editing in «Доступы» (047), with one 010 change-audit row per change; a change of `module.reward` or `course.clubInCompletion` on a published course SHALL go through the 024 change dialog (EARS-31) by a `learning_rules_editor`.

## Invariants

- 023 adds no structure table, no rule model and no stored status; every course state on a school screen is the 024 evaluator's (EARS-3).
- A preset's name never reaches a doctor read (EARS-11, EARS-23).
- Only a doctor with a done course of the school writes a review or a mentoring request; only `published` reviews are shown (EARS-16, EARS-18, EARS-19).
- `module.completed` reaches 025 only WHILE the module's reward includes points; a module document is requested at most once per doctor and module (EARS-14).
- The course certificate is requested for the whole course only, whatever the module rewards (EARS-2, EARS-14).
- No price, purchase or money text on any school or course screen (EARS-23).
