# 024 — The course path, lesson steps and the rules constructor (Requirements)

## Outcomes

- A doctor learns in **lessons that are sets of steps** worth about an academic hour, never one video, and always sees **one continuous course path**: modules are its sections, steps its nodes, a lesson a run of consecutive steps, each module closed by its club node and the course by its document node.
- Every step's state — done · you are here · open · locked with the reason · reachable by passing a test on what was skipped — is **computed** by one evaluator from stored facts and the rules in force; the website and the mobile app read the same path data and differ only in presentation.
- **Every rule is a setting**: completion, unlocking, order, visibility of locked items, tests, test-out and repeat are layered data with inheritance and presets, edited in `apps/admin` without a release; only the element-type catalog, the condition-type catalog and the evaluator are code.
- **Nothing a doctor did is silently lost**: facts are append-only; a rule change recomputes statuses, keeps earned completions unless the change requires them again, and tells the doctor why a status changed.
- Feature **025** receives `lesson.completed` and `module.completed` exactly when the evaluator's status first turns done, and decides every point.

## Scope

**In:**

- The **structure model** in `packages/learning`: school, course, module, lesson and step rows (the minimal school row — direction, leading expert, title, slug, project reference, state — and the minimal course and module rows — school, project, slug, title, order, state — that the path needs), the lesson's composition from the element-type catalog, and the admin API that writes them.
- The **element-type catalog** and the **condition-type catalog** (code), with each type's «viewed» definition and its default.
- The **rule model**: setting layers platform → school → course → module → lesson → step, the resolver with the source level, the completion-rule grammar, unlock, order, visibility, test, test-out and repeat settings, three seeded presets.
- **Progress facts**, the **evaluator**, the status projection with its reasons, and the emission of `lesson.completed` / `module.completed` to 025.
- The **doctor screens** on `apps/doctor`: the course path page `/courses/{courseSlug}` and the step screen; the guest gate; tests, corrective material, test-out, repeat, ratings and feedback; the same reads for the mobile app.
- The **admin screens** in `apps/admin`: lesson composition, the rules editor (presets, overrides, the «inherited from» display, platform defaults and preset contents) and the live-course change dialog; the learning-rules staff role in «Доступы» (047).

### Out of scope

- **The school screen, the school's descriptive fields, graduate reviews, mentoring and internship, the «скоро» state, the course page header and the structure editor screens** — feature **023**, over the school, course and module rows of EARS-1: the course header (description, author, document, rules summary) on the route of EARS-14 and the admin screens that create and order schools, courses and modules. 023 adds no school table; 024 writes these rows through its admin API (EARS-1).
- **Points** — accrual, spend and the points plate are feature **025**; 024 reports facts only (EARS-12).
- **Issuing and verifying the course document** — features **038** / **037**; the document node shows the course's completion state and links to 038's document.
- **The club event itself** — 005 / 020 own the live event and its attendance fact; the club node reads that fact (EARS-6).
- **Content production routing and the lesson template's duration and composition** — feature **040** (lead decision 5: the theory share is a template default, not a platform rule).
- **The mobile application** — its own track; it consumes the reads and writes of EARS-10, EARS-13, EARS-15, EARS-18, EARS-20, EARS-23 (EARS-25).
- **The game layer** (streaks, hearts, leagues, XP) — the app's layer, never shown on the website.

## Constraints

- **Rules are data.** No threshold, percentage, order, unlock condition, attempt count, cooldown or visibility is a literal in code; each is read from the resolved layers (EARS-5). The only defaults in code are the seed of the platform layer and of the presets, migrated as rows.
- **Facts are append-only.** No UPDATE or DELETE reaches a fact row — enforced by a database trigger (ADR-0003 §2.7); a status is never stored as the truth, the projection is rebuildable from facts and rules.
- **Server-side evidence.** Test answers are graded on the server and the correct answers never reach the client before submission; a client never asserts a completion, only facts the evaluator reads.
- **No unlock by payment.** Doctors buy nothing (D10); no rule, condition or setting refers to a price or a payment.
- **Copy from data.** Dates, counts, durations, thresholds and the rule sentences are built from data and the resolved rules, never fixed strings (lead decision 8); no interface text says «бесплатно», speaks of money or who pays, or says «проект».
- **One package.** `packages/learning` owns the model, the evaluator and the UI blocks; `apps/doctor` adds only route files and host-config fields, `apps/admin` only its route files over the package's admin blocks (ADR-0013 §A1).

## Prior decisions

- **ADR-0016 §4:** school, course, module, lesson are project outputs and carry `project_id`; the lesson is the funding unit. **§5:** a school hangs on a direction (`directions`), several schools per direction and one per leading expert (expert per §3); a course always lives in a school. **§6:** 024 writes no posting. **§8:** structure rows and facts are `doctor`; rule layers, presets and authoring rows `admin-only`. **§9:** a rule change absorbs, never rewrites, facts.
- **ADR-0003 §2.7:** facts carry a unique `event_id` and a trigger refuses UPDATE/DELETE.
- **ADR-0013 §A1:** one shared package per capability — `packages/learning`, row «Learning path — course page and lesson» of the capability registry.
- **ADR-0014:** this triplet formalises `024-product.md`; every clause `realizes:` a US.
- **025 EARS-7, EARS-8, EARS-32:** the fact types `lesson.completed { mode }` (ref lesson) and `module.completed { mode }` (ref module) are registered in 025's catalog; 024 records them through `PointsEngine` in its own transaction.
- **Owner D16:** earned progress on a rule change — default «keep», the author may choose «require again» per change. **Owner D17:** test-out is OFF in «ДПО / аккредитация», ON in «Путь (Duolingo)»; a per-course override may switch it on.
- **Lead, 2026-11-12:** the condition-type catalog and «viewed» defaults are the PRD list; on a draft course its editors change rules, on a published course only a holder of the learning-rules grant, seeing the impact and the D16 choice, every change audited by 010, no second-person approval; the authoring screens are in scope, built from `@ds/design-system` with no canvas and no Stage A.

## Event Model

**Commands** — `CreateSchool`, `CreateCourse`, `CreateModule`, `CreateLesson`, `UpdateLesson`, `AddStep`, `UpdateStep`, `ReorderSteps`, `RemoveStep`, `PublishLesson`, `UnpublishLesson` (admin); `SetRuleLayer`, `ClearRuleSetting`, `ApplyPreset`, `UpdatePreset`, `PreviewRuleChange`, `ConfirmRuleChange { earnedProgress: keep | require-again }` (admin); `RecordProgress`, `StartAttempt`, `SubmitAttempt`, `StartTestOut`, `RateLesson`, `SendFeedback` (doctor).

**Events** — `LessonPublished`, `LessonUnpublished`, `RuleLayerChanged`, `PresetApplied` (each a 010 change-audit row); `ProgressRecorded`, `AttemptSubmitted`, `TestOutPassed`, `CompletionKept` (append-only facts); `StepCompleted`, `LessonCompleted`, `ModuleCompleted`, `CourseCompleted`, `TestPassed`, `StatusChanged { cause: fact | rule-change }` (evaluator transitions); to 025: `lesson.completed`, `module.completed`.

**Read models** — the course path; the lesson with its steps and rule sentences; the step payload; the attempt result with corrective material; the admin lesson composition, the effective rules of a scope with their source levels, the rule-change impact.

**Policies** — «a status is computed, never asserted»; «the nearest layer wins»; «a fact is never changed or removed»; «a rule change keeps earned completions unless the author requires them again»; «025 hears a completion once, when it first turns done»; «a guest is never served gated content»; «an unpublished lesson is not on the doctor's path».

## EARS requirements

> Flat numbering per ADR-0006 §4. Every clause realizes one or more PRD stories and is covered by `024-scenarios.feature`.

- **EARS-1** _(realizes: US-1, US-18)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL keep the structure as data in `packages/learning`: a school (direction of ADR-0016 §5, leading expert of ADR-0016 §3 — one school per expert and direction —, title, slug, project reference, state `draft` | `published`), a course (its school, never null, project reference, slug, title, state `draft` | `published`), its ordered modules, each module's ordered lessons and each lesson's ordered steps; a lesson SHALL carry its module (never null), project reference, title, description and teaser, and a step its element type from the catalog of EARS-2, title, expected duration in minutes and the type's payload; the lesson's duration SHALL be the sum of its steps' durations; every row SHALL declare the storefront ownership `doctor` (ADR-0016 §8), no course SHALL exist outside a school and no lesson outside a module; the admin API that creates a course SHALL take its school and refuse a missing or unknown one with a field error.
- **EARS-2** _(realizes: US-18, US-1, US-15)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL hold the **element-type catalog** as code in `packages/learning` with the initial types `video` (a series episode), `microlearning` (cards), `download` (a downloadable material, optionally a partner's), `podcast`, `test` and `clinical-case`, each declaring its payload schema, the progress facts it records and its **«viewed» definition** with its default — `video` watched to 100 % (owner D15), `podcast` listened to 90 %, `download` opened once, `microlearning` every card viewed, `clinical-case` reached its end, `test` passed at its threshold — where a percentage is a setting of EARS-5; a step of a type the catalog does not declare SHALL be refused with a field error and nothing stored.
- **EARS-3** _(realizes: US-18, US-15)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL hold the **condition-type catalog** as code with the initial types `viewed` (a step's «viewed» definition of EARS-2), `watched-at-least { percent }`, `opened`, `listened-at-least { percent }`, `test-passed { test }`, `club-attended { module }`, `manual-mark`, `all-steps-done` and `lessons-done`; a rule naming a condition the catalog does not declare SHALL be refused; adding a condition type SHALL be a code change, adding a combination or a number SHALL NOT.
- **EARS-4** _(realizes: US-1)_ — WHEN an author publishes a lesson, THE SYSTEM SHALL refuse with `409 PUBLISH_REQUIREMENTS_NOT_MET` and field errors naming each missing item — at least one step, every step's required payload, the exit test and, where the resolved rules require one, the entry test — and otherwise SHALL publish the whole lesson; WHILE a lesson is not published, THE SYSTEM SHALL leave it off every doctor read, and an unpublished lesson SHALL keep its doctors' facts.
- **EARS-5** _(realizes: US-14, US-13)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL resolve every setting through the layers platform → school → course → module → lesson → step, the nearest layer holding the setting winning, a layer with no row skipped; the resolver SHALL return, for each setting, its value, the level it comes from and, when that level's value was written by a preset, the preset's name; the platform layer SHALL hold a value for every setting so resolution always ends in a value.
- **EARS-6** _(realizes: US-15, US-3)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL express a completion rule as a tree of `all`, `any` and `n-of { n }` over conditions of EARS-3, validated on write (every referenced step, test and module inside the rule's scope, `n` between 1 and the number of children, depth at most 4); a step's default completion SHALL be its type's `viewed`, a lesson's `all-steps-done` and its exit test `test-passed`, a module's `lessons-done` — and `club-attended` only WHILE both the course's closing-club setting and its club-in-completion setting are on (023 lead decision: off by default, the club being a live scheduled event) —, a course's every module done; a rule failing validation SHALL be refused with field errors and nothing changed.
- **EARS-7** _(realizes: US-15, US-16)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL offer the unlock settings `immediate`, `after-previous`, `from-date { instant }` and `expression` (a completion-rule tree over other items of the same course), the order setting `strict` | `free` of a module and the locked-visibility setting `hidden` | `locked`, each inheriting by EARS-5; no unlock setting SHALL refer to a price or a payment; the registration gate of EARS-22 SHALL apply on top of every unlock.
- **EARS-8** _(realizes: US-4, US-5, US-7)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL offer the test settings pass threshold (whole percent), attempts (a number or unlimited), the pause between attempts (minutes), entry test required (yes/no), entry test counts toward completion (yes/no) and the number of questions drawn per attempt, plus the settings test-out allowed (yes/no), the pause after a failed test-out (minutes), repeat of a done lesson allowed (yes/no) and the course settings delivery mode (`online` | `offline` | `open`, the `mode` 025 reads, default `online`), module size (default 6 lessons, read by 023's editor), closing club (default on) and club in completion (default off).
- **EARS-9** _(realizes: US-13, US-5)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL seed three presets as data: «Путь (Duolingo)» — strict order, unlock after the previous item, test-out allowed, repeat allowed, locked items shown with a lock; «Открытый курс» — every item immediate, free order, a lesson done when all its steps are viewed and the exit test passed; «ДПО / аккредитация» — a lesson done when all steps are viewed and the exit test is at or above 80 %, the entry test required, strict order inside a module, test-out not allowed (owner D17); WHEN an author applies a preset to a school or a course, THE SYSTEM SHALL write the preset's values as that layer in one action and record the preset's name, keep every override of the layers below, and leave every other layer unchanged; a later change of the preset's contents SHALL NOT change a layer it was applied to before.
- **EARS-10** _(realizes: US-1, US-12, US-19)_ — WHEN a signed-in doctor's client reports progress on a step through `POST /v1/me/learning/facts` — watched or listened position, opened, a card viewed, a case end reached —, THE SYSTEM SHALL validate it against the step type's fact schema, store it as an append-only fact with the client's event id, the person, the step, the occurrence instant and the source (`web` | `app`), answer a replayed event id as a duplicate without a second row, and refuse a fact for a step the doctor may not open by EARS-11 or EARS-22 with `403 LEARNING_STEP_LOCKED` and nothing stored; positions SHALL count toward «viewed» only up to the step's real media duration.
- **EARS-11** _(realizes: US-2, US-3, US-19)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL compute every step, lesson, module and course status of a doctor — `done`, `current`, `open`, `locked { reason }` or `test-out-reachable` — with one evaluator from the doctor's facts and the rules resolved at the time of computing; `current` SHALL be the first step in path order that is open and not done; a `locked` reason SHALL name the unmet unlock (the previous item, the date, the expression, registration); WHEN a computed status differs from the doctor's projected status, THE SYSTEM SHALL update the rebuildable projection with the new status, the instant and its cause (`fact` or the rule change of EARS-31).
- **EARS-12** _(realizes: US-20)_ — WHEN a doctor's lesson or module status first turns `done`, THE SYSTEM SHALL record `lesson.completed` or — WHILE the module's resolved reward of 023 includes points — `module.completed` with the course's delivery mode as `mode` through `PointsEngine.record(tx, fact)` in the transaction of the fact or rule change that caused it, its event id derived from the person and the item so a later re-completion replays as a duplicate, and publish after commit; it SHALL also publish `StepCompleted`, `TestPassed` and `CourseCompleted` in-process; 024 SHALL NOT name a rule, an amount or a denomination, and a status turning back from `done` SHALL record nothing toward 025.
- **EARS-13** _(realizes: US-2, US-12)_ — WHEN any caller reads `GET /v1/learning/courses/{courseSlug}/path`, THE SYSTEM SHALL answer the published course as one ordered path: its modules as sections, each lesson as a run of consecutive step nodes with its title, duration and step count, each module's club node WHILE closing club is on, and the document node last; every node SHALL carry its computed state of EARS-11 for the signed-in doctor — for a guest computed with no facts — and items whose resolved visibility is `hidden` and that are locked SHALL be omitted; an unknown or unpublished course SHALL be answered `404`.
- **EARS-14** _(realizes: US-2, US-12)_ — WHEN a doctor or a guest opens `doctor.school/courses/{courseSlug}`, THE SYSTEM SHALL render the course path block of `packages/learning` per `design-source/doctor-course.dc.html` (v16): the «snake» of step nodes with module sections, lesson segments, club and document nodes, each node's state mark, «Вы здесь» on the current step with «Продолжить», the lesson in view highlighted in the «Модули и уроки» outline, a locked node's reason in the doctor's words (e.g. «Шаг откроется, когда будут пройдены предыдущие.»), the points plate and no game layer; WHILE the path loads it SHALL show the canvas loading state, WHEN the course has no published lesson the empty state «Уроки появятся здесь, когда курс будет опубликован.», and WHEN the read fails an alert with «Повторить».
- **EARS-15** _(realizes: US-1, US-3)_ — WHEN a doctor opens a step from the path, THE SYSTEM SHALL render the step as its own screen per `design-source/doctor-lesson.dc.html` (v18) at `/courses/{courseSlug}/steps/{stepId}`: the lesson header «Урок N · K шагов · <duration>», the strip of the lesson's steps with their states, the step body of EARS-16 or EARS-18, the step's completion sentence and the lesson's completion sentence of EARS-17, «Вы здесь» on the current step, and the way to the next step WHEN the order allows it; a step that is `locked` SHALL open as its locked reason and no content.
- **EARS-16** _(realizes: US-1, US-9, US-10)_ — WHEN a step screen renders a content step, THE SYSTEM SHALL render it by type — `video` with «Смотреть видео» and the watched share («Просмотрено 35 %»), `podcast` with «Слушать подкаст» and the listened share, playable alongside the lesson's other steps WHILE it is marked as the lesson's companion, `download` with «Скачать», `microlearning` one card at a time with «Предыдущая карточка» / «Следующая карточка», `clinical-case` through its stages to its end — each recording its facts by EARS-10; a partner's `download` SHALL carry the mark «Партнёрский материал · <partner>» from the 032 organisation registry, and no partner or advertising content SHALL appear inside any other step type.
- **EARS-17** _(realizes: US-3)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL generate each step's and lesson's completion sentence from its resolved completion rule and settings — «Шаг засчитывается, когда видео просмотрено на 100 %», «Урок засчитывается, когда пройдены все шаги и тест сдан не ниже 80 %» — from one sentence builder in `packages/learning` per condition type, so a changed rule changes the sentence without a release and no sentence is a fixed string.
- **EARS-18** _(realizes: US-4, US-6)_ — WHEN a doctor starts a test step, THE SYSTEM SHALL start an attempt through `POST /v1/me/learning/tests/{testId}/attempts` with the resolved number of questions and no correct answers, refuse a start beyond the resolved attempts or inside the pause with `409 LEARNING_ATTEMPT_UNAVAILABLE` naming when the next attempt opens, and WHEN the doctor submits, grade it on the server, store the attempt as a fact with its score and answers, and show «Тест сдан: 90 % при пороге 80 %» or «Тест не сдан: 60 % при пороге 80 %. Пройденные шаги сохранены.» with the attempts left; WHILE the resolved rules require an entry test, the lesson's other steps SHALL stay locked until it is submitted; a wrong answer SHALL lower the score and SHALL record nothing that removes points.
- **EARS-19** _(realizes: US-6)_ — WHEN a submitted attempt has a wrong answer whose question carries corrective material — a step of the same course or an explanation text set in the composition of EARS-28 —, THE SYSTEM SHALL offer it after the result as «Разбор по этой ошибке» with «Посмотреть разбор», per question, without changing the attempt's score.
- **EARS-20** _(realizes: US-5)_ — WHILE test-out is allowed for an item and the doctor has not done it, THE SYSTEM SHALL mark the item `test-out-reachable` with «Перейти сюда — сдать тест по пропущенному»; WHEN the doctor passes the item's test-out test at or above its threshold, THE SYSTEM SHALL store a test-out fact that satisfies the completion of every skipped step and lesson up to that item, and the path SHALL recompute; WHEN the score is below the threshold, THE SYSTEM SHALL change nothing and leave the doctor at their current step, apply the resolved pause after a failed test-out, state on the screen when the next test-out attempt is possible from that pause, and offer to continue from the current step.
- **EARS-21** _(realizes: US-7)_ — WHILE repeat is allowed for a done lesson, THE SYSTEM SHALL offer «Повторить» on the lesson; WHEN the doctor repeats it, THE SYSTEM SHALL record new facts and attempts as usual, keep the lesson `done` whatever those facts show, and record nothing new toward 025 (EARS-12); WHILE repeat is not allowed, a done lesson's steps SHALL stay readable without new facts.
- **EARS-22** _(realizes: US-8)_ — WHEN a guest opens the path or a step, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the course path, the lesson description, the step list with durations and the teaser, and in place of each content step the gate «Чтобы пройти урок, нужна регистрация. Вернём вас сюда.» leading to registration (021) with this step as the return target; every step-payload read and every write of EARS-10, EARS-18, EARS-20 and EARS-23 SHALL refuse a guest with `401` and serve no gated content.
- **EARS-23** _(realizes: US-11)_ — WHEN a signed-in doctor rates a lesson with one to five stars, THE SYSTEM SHALL store one rating per doctor and lesson, replaced by a later rating; WHEN the doctor sends feedback on a partner's step, THE SYSTEM SHALL store it with the person, the step and the instant, state on the screen that the feedback is read by Doctor.School staff and reaches the partner only as an anonymised aggregate, list it to staff on the lesson's composition screen (EARS-28), and never pass the doctor's identity or the individual text to the partner.
- **EARS-24** _(realizes: US-19, US-17)_ — WHEN a doctor's lesson status changed because of a rule change (EARS-31), THE SYSTEM SHALL show on the lesson and its path node the reason built from that change — what changed and what is now needed — until the doctor next completes the lesson; no fact of the doctor SHALL be changed or removed.
- **EARS-25** _(realizes: US-12)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL serve the path, the lesson, the step payload, the facts intake, the attempts, test-out and ratings through the same REST endpoints with Zod contracts in `packages/schemas` to the website and the mobile app, so one doctor has one set of facts and statuses on both; WHILE the viewport is a phone, the path page and every step screen SHALL work without horizontal page scroll with every control keyboard-reachable, and each SHALL pass the platform's `playwright-axe` gate.
- **EARS-26** _(realizes: US-1, US-2)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL build every date, count, duration, threshold and promise on the path and step screens from data (lead decision 8), and no text of these screens SHALL say «бесплатно» in any form, speak of money or who pays, or say «проект».
- **EARS-27** _(realizes: US-2)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL serve the path and the step screens only on the doctor storefront: the Academy host SHALL have no `/courses/{courseSlug}` path or step route and SHALL answer them with its not-found page.
- **EARS-28** _(realizes: US-1, US-10, US-6, US-18)_ — WHEN a staff member opens a module's lessons in `apps/admin`, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the lesson composition screen built from `@ds/design-system`: create a lesson (title, description, teaser, project), add a step of any catalog type with its payload — media of the 012 media contract, a file, a test with questions, options, the correct option and per-question corrective material, a clinical case's stages, a partner organisation of the 032 registry for a partner download —, reorder and remove steps, mark a podcast as the lesson's companion, see the lesson's total duration, preview a step as EARS-15 renders it, publish and unpublish by EARS-4, and read the doctors' feedback of EARS-23; a refused write SHALL change nothing and name its field errors.
- **EARS-29** _(realizes: US-13, US-14, US-15, US-16)_ — WHEN a staff member opens the rules of a platform, school, course, module, lesson or step scope in `apps/admin`, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the rules editor built from `@ds/design-system`: every setting of EARS-6…8 with its effective value and «Унаследовано от: <level>» (and the preset's name) or «Задано здесь», an override and «Вернуть унаследованное» per setting, the completion-rule builder of `all` / `any` / `N из M` over catalog conditions, «Применить пресет» at a school or course, and the contents of each preset on the platform scope; a refused rule SHALL change nothing and name its field errors.
- **EARS-30** _(realizes: US-17, US-13)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL let rules of a draft course be changed by the staff who may edit that course and rules of a published course, the platform layer and the presets only by a holder of the staff role `learning_rules_editor` («Редактор правил обучения») granted in «Доступы» (047) with the second factor; every admin learning endpoint SHALL require the dedicated admin session, CSRF, a canonical idempotency key and, except for create, the current `If-Match` version, refusing a stale one with `412 PRECONDITION_FAILED`; every change SHALL leave one 010 change-audit row under the acting staff member; no change SHALL require a second person's approval.
- **EARS-31** _(realizes: US-17, US-19)_ — WHEN a `learning_rules_editor` saves a rule change on a published course, THE SYSTEM SHALL first show the change dialog with the number of doctors whose statuses would change — computed by the evaluator over their facts under the old and the new rules without writing — and the choice «Сохранить заработанные зачёты» (preselected, owner D16) or «Потребовать заново»; WHEN the change is confirmed, THE SYSTEM SHALL apply it, under «keep» store a `completion-kept` fact for every item that was `done` and would no longer be, recompute the affected doctors' statuses by EARS-11 with the change as the cause, and emit the transitions of EARS-12; no release and no data migration SHALL be needed, and no fact SHALL be changed or removed.

## Invariants

- No status is stored as the truth; every status is the evaluator's result over facts and resolved rules, and the projection can be rebuilt (EARS-11).
- No fact row is ever updated or deleted; a rule change only adds facts (EARS-10, EARS-31).
- Every setting resolves to exactly one value with a named source level (EARS-5).
- 025 hears each lesson and module completion of a doctor at most once (EARS-12); 024 writes no posting.
- A guest is never served a step payload; a locked step is never served its content (EARS-10, EARS-15, EARS-22).
- An unpublished lesson never appears on a doctor's path (EARS-4, EARS-13).
- No rule, setting or text refers to a price or a payment for the doctor (EARS-7, EARS-26).
- Every rule change on a published course is made by a `learning_rules_editor` and leaves one 010 audit row (EARS-30).
