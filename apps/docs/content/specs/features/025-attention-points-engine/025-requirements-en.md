# 025 — Attention points engine (Requirements)

## Outcomes

- Every doctor has **exactly one attention-points account** that the doctor storefront, the Academy and the mobile app all read and post to through one API; the same balance is true everywhere by construction, never by synchronisation.
- The account carries three **denominations**: **Pul** (the only spendable one, never exchangeable to money), **Bre** (reputation) and **Con** (awareness, only grows). The fixed weight 1 Con = 2 Bre is used only by the leaderboard and attention reporting.
- Every movement is an **immutable double-entry posting**; a balance is a fold over postings; a correction is a compensating posting; every posting names the **rule and rule version** that produced it, so experiments stay comparable and sponsor reports stay reconcilable after re-tuning.
- Every accrual amount, price, cap, threshold and formula is a **configurable, versioned parameter with a default**, changed through the admin API without a release.
- The same (doctor, content item, action) is **accrued at most once**, whichever storefront or device reported it, including the mobile app's offline sync; mistakes never remove points.
- Pul is **reserved on sign-up** for a priced item, **debited only on confirmed attendance**, and **released** on a cancel, a no-show or an item cancelled by the platform — so there is no negative balance and no refund procedure.
- The **advance** is a per-item flag, identical for every doctor; an arrival link records the source only. An advance is an obligation netted from future Pul earnings, capped at 150 Pul per person, never a negative balance and never a block on open content.
- A consented, monthly, per-specialty **leaderboard read model** ranks 2·Con + Bre earned in the calendar month; the Pul balance never ranks.
- Feature **036** receives **attention aggregates** per project and output built from postings with their rule versions, with no per-doctor rows.

## Scope

**In:**

- The **ledger**: person accounts and the system accounts double entry needs (ADR-0016 design §2.11), postings (§2.12) with the denomination, the fact-level `event_id` unique with its `leg`, the dedupe key and the rule version; the immutability trigger and monthly partitions of ADR-0003 §3, mirroring the shipped `audit_ledger`.
- The **accrual engine**: consuming the accrual-source domain facts listed in the Event Model, evaluating the active rule version, writing postings and emitting `PointsCredited`.
- The **rule book**: versioned accrual rules, event prices and gating modes, the advance cap, the referral cap, the presence share, the no-show window, the concurrent-reserve limit and the leaderboard formula, each with the PRD default.
- The **spend side**: the gating decision, reserve / settle / release over a **price list of any item kind** (events, courses, trainings, internships, offline events, …), the advance and its obligation, settlement from future earnings.
- The **read side** over the API: the doctor's own balances and history, the gating decision for one doctor and one item, the monthly leaderboard read model, the attention aggregates for 036.
- The **admin API** for rules, settings, the price list and compensating corrections, the catalog and journal reads and the starter-bonus grant, each change audited.
- The **erasure seam**: closing and freezing a person account.

**Out (each with its owner):**

- **Every screen.** The event page's price line, advance progress, threshold hint and the money statement «бесплатно для врача» decoupled from `pulCost` — feature **020**, aligned in [#3801](https://github.com/doctor-school/ds-platform/issues/3801); the registration promise and the credit on the success state — feature **021**, aligned in [#3801](https://github.com/doctor-school/ds-platform/issues/3801); the account's three balances, open advance and posting history — feature **022**, Issue [#1791](https://github.com/doctor-school/ds-platform/issues/1791); the consent-gated specialty leaderboard block and the consent toggle — feature **018**, Issue [#1495](https://github.com/doctor-school/ds-platform/issues/1495); the learning screens — feature **024**; the mobile app — its own track (functional map, owner decision 2026-08-22), consuming this API.
- **The admin screens** for rules, settings, the price list, corrections and the starter-bonus grant — the fact-type catalog view, the structured rule editor, the simulation of a draft rule against the journal. 025 ships the admin **API** only; the admin UI is deferred to the follow-up Issue [#3815](https://github.com/doctor-school/ds-platform/issues/3815), which owns the `apps/admin` surface and its browser verification.
- **The waiting list** on limited-seat items — event registration (**020** / **005**); 025 only refuses a reserve beyond the concurrent-reserve limit (EARS-21).
- **Attendance confirmation itself** — the presence beats and the attendance fact are the room's and the event registration's: the room reports `attendance.confirmed` when the event ends (EARS-9); 025 consumes the confirmed-attendance fact and owns only the presence share setting it is computed against (EARS-9).
- **Phone verification and profile verification** — the account's (feature **022**, over **003**); 025 consumes the facts.
- **The producers' emit calls** — each producing feature (021, 022, 024, 020 / the room, and the future owners of courses, trainings and internships) registers its fact types and calls `record` in its own slice; 025 owns the catalog mechanism, not their domain events.
- **Money** — sponsor billing, invoices, the money side of attention aggregates and named sponsor reports under a separate art. 9 consent — feature **036**.
- **Next-wave sinks**, Pul for partner or promotional content, an advertising coefficient, admin anomaly analytics — not at launch (PRD Out of scope).

## Constraints

- **One ledger family.** No balance column anywhere, no second ledger, no per-storefront account; the money unit of the same family is 036's and is not written here.
- **Postings are immutable.** No UPDATE or DELETE reaches a posting row — enforced in the database, not only in code; a correction is a new posting that names the one it reverses.
- **Every number is a parameter.** No accrual amount, price, cap, share or weight is a literal in code; each is read from the active rule, setting or price-list version and stamped on the posting it produces.
- **Rules depend only on attention.** No rule input reads a sponsor, a medical-representative or partner arrival source, a prescription, a sale or a per-doctor sponsor-funded value (323-FZ art. 74, AIPM — PRD Compliance guardrails); such an arrival source is recorded on the posting as attribution only. The one arrival a rule reads is the doctor-to-doctor referral link of EARS-11 (`referral.attributed`).
- **Pul is never money.** No API field, problem detail or admin text expresses a Pul amount in money, calls a Pul price «бесплатно», or names points «баллы» in the НМО sense; no field derives a «free» statement from a zero Pul cost.
- **Lessons never cost Pul** and events never accrue Pul; an НМО-accredited event is always open.
- **No legacy points concept.** Nothing migrates into the ledger from the old platform; the starter bonus (EARS-43) is a new grant, not a conversion.
- **Server-side evidence only.** Lesson and module credit is accrued only on 024's computed completion facts, event credit only on the server-side attendance fact; a client-asserted completion is never an accrual source.
- **Producers state what happened.** A producer never names a rule, an amount, a denomination or a dedupe key; what a fact is worth is the rule book's.

## Prior decisions

- **ADR-0016 §6 + design §2.11–§2.12:** one append-only ledger family; ledger accounts per subject (`person` · `project_fund` · `organisation` · `system`) with unit `attention_points` | `money`; immutable double-entry postings carrying project, output, `rule_id` / `rule_version`, the attribution subject (organisation, member) and `reverses_posting_id`; a balance is a fold; on erasure the account is closed and frozen and postings are never rewritten.
- **ADR-0003 §2.7 / §3:** append-only with a unique `event_id`, a trigger refusing UPDATE/DELETE, and monthly partitions — `audit_ledger` (`packages/db/src/schema/audit-ledger.ts`) is the shipped template.
- **ADR-0016 §6 and design §2.11–§2.12 as rewritten in this PR** (the ledger is not in production, so the ADR is rewritten inline): a posting with unit `attention_points` carries a **`denomination`** (`pul` · `bre` · `con`), so one person keeps **one** attention-points account; every posting carries the **`event_id` of the one domain fact** that produced it and is unique on (`event_id`, `leg`), the `leg` naming the posting within its fact (denomination + entry kind); global dedupe lives in the non-partitioned **`points_posting_keys`**, one row per fact keyed by its `event_id` and its dedupe key (doctor + content item + action), inserted in the same transaction before the fact's postings. That is how the points ledger satisfies ADR-0003 §3's `event_id` dedupe and immutability over monthly partitions; ADR-0003 itself is unchanged.
- **ADR-0009:** the leaderboard-dissemination consent (152-FZ art. 10.1) is one more versioned per-purpose record in `consent_records`; 025 invents no consent store.
- **ADR-0001:** the doctor reads only their own account; the admin API requires an admin role; consumers post facts service-to-service, never with a doctor's token on another doctor's account.
- **Owner, 2026-11-06 (Issue #1794):** the advance is a per-event flag for every doctor; every accrual is a configurable parameter and every posting records its rule version; no legacy points concept.
- **Owner, 2026-11-09 (Issue #3835):** one universal rule engine — see the production amendment below for the verbatim decisions.

## Production amendment — one universal rule engine (2026-11-09, #3835)

The ledger core (#3807, migration 0058) and the rule book with its accrual intake (#3808, migration 0059 — the 21 seeded version-1 rule rows, the `issuance` account, the rule-version immutability trigger and `POST /v1/admin/points/rules/:ruleId/versions`) run in production since release-2026.11.07-1; no producer calls the intake yet. This amendment overrides: **EARS-3** (a closed action evaluated against its one `accrual.<action>` rule), **EARS-4** (the dedupe key «(person, content item, action)»), **EARS-25** (one parameter schema per hard-coded rule id, the closed action enum, `points-rule-unknown`); the Event Model command `AccrueFromFact` (replaced by `RecordFact`) and the parameter shape of `PublishRuleVersion`; the Scope bullets «accrual engine» and «rule book»; the Outcomes line on «(doctor, content item, action)» (the same outcome now holds through a rule's `oncePer` limit); the Prior-decisions wording «dedupe key (doctor + content item + action)» (the key is now the applied rule's limit scope); the Invariants lines on the dedupe key and on one `points_posting_keys` row per fact; and the Verification rows 3, 4 and 25. It adds **EARS-32…46**. The running-production baseline is retained below. The clauses not yet running in production — EARS-5…11, 13…19, 21, 23 and 26 — are rewritten inline in the body as seeded rules and settings of this engine, keeping every PRD amount. **No EARS number moves**: every number keeps its subject, so Issues #3809–#3816 keep their references (design production amendment, «Clause map»). The migration is additive over 0058 and 0059 (EARS-44). Design mechanics: design production amendment of the same date.

**Amended clauses.**

- **EARS-3 amended — a recorded fact is evaluated against every matching rule.** _(realizes: US-2, US-3, US-11, US-12, US-15)_ When `record` accepts a fact (EARS-33), the system shall select every rule whose version active at the fact's `occurredAt` lists the fact's type in its trigger, is `active` and is valid at `occurredAt` (EARS-37); evaluate each one's condition (EARS-34) and limits (EARS-36); resolve stacking (EARS-38); apply the debit-side effects before the credits (EARS-35); and write each applied rule as **one ledger fact** with `event_id = <fact event id>#<rule id>`, its own `points_posting_keys` row (amended EARS-4) and one immutable double-entry posting per non-zero denomination and entry kind, each posting carrying that ledger fact's `event_id`, a `leg`, the denomination, the applied rule's `rule_id` and `rule_version`, the fact type as its `action`, the project and output when the fact names them, the attribution subject when one is recorded, `occurred_at` and `recorded_at`. The fact's journal row (EARS-40) and the postings of every applied rule are written in one transaction; after COMMIT the system shall emit one `PointsCredited` per credited person carrying that person's credit postings — still the only event from which a consumer may state a credited amount.
- **EARS-4 amended — dedupe on the fact and on the rule's limit scope.** _(realizes: US-4, US-1)_ When a fact arrives whose `event_id` is already in the fact journal — a retry, or the mobile app's offline sync replaying it — the system shall evaluate nothing, write nothing and answer with the original outcome (`duplicate`, referencing the original applications). When a rule application's dedupe key `<limit key>:<oncePer scope values>` (EARS-36; the limit key defaults to the rule id) already has a `points_posting_keys` row, that rule shall be skipped and traced as `limit-reached` while the other rules of the fact still apply; a fact all of whose otherwise applicable rules stop on an existing `oncePer` key answers `duplicate`. So one lesson reported by the doctor storefront, the Academy and an offline sync under three event ids credits once under the `oncePer(subject, ref)` lesson rule. Both uniquenesses shall be enforced by database constraints, not only by a read-before-write.
- **EARS-25 amended — one rule schema, any rule id.** _(realizes: US-11, US-12)_ When an admin publishes a version through `POST /v1/admin/points/rules/:ruleId/versions`, the system shall accept a new admin-chosen rule id (a lowercase dotted slug outside the `setting.` namespace and the system id `manual-correction`) as well as an existing one; validate the body against the **one** rule schema — a `rule` (trigger, condition, effect, limits, validity, active, stacking, label) or, for a `setting.` id, that setting's schema (EARS-39), discriminated by `kind` — and against the catalog: every trigger names a registered fact type (EARS-32); the condition type-checks (EARS-34); the effect kind and denominations are legal (EARS-35, EARS-12), with no Pul credit on a fact type the catalog marks as not earning Pul; every limit scope field exists on every trigger fact type and a credit names a limit or an explicit `unlimited` (EARS-36); no amount exceeds `setting.effect.maxAmount`; a version's `kind` equals that of the rule id's earlier versions. It shall refuse a failure with RFC 7807 problem `points-rule-parameters-invalid` (422) carrying the field path, refuse a past `effectiveFrom` (`points-rule-effective-from-past`, 422), refuse a 0059 id the engine retired (design, «Clause map») with `points-rule-retired` (422), store the version as a new immutable row effective from now or a later `effectiveFrom`, audit it, answer with any stacking warning (EARS-38), and apply it to facts recorded afterwards without a release; past postings keep the version they carry and past facts are not re-evaluated (EARS-42).

**Added clauses.**

- **EARS-32** _(realizes: US-15, US-11)_ — The system shall hold a developer-registered **fact-type catalog** in `packages/schemas/src/points/`, each entry a stable dotted past-tense type (e.g. `lesson.completed`), its subject (the person whose account it concerns), its ref kind (what it is about — `lesson`, `module`, `item`, `person`, or none), a strict Zod schema of typed attributes, and whether a rule on it may credit Pul; registered predicates (EARS-34) are part of the same readable vocabulary. A unit test over the whole catalog (`packages/schemas/src/points/fact-types.spec.ts`, run in CI) shall refuse any attribute or predicate that names or carries a sponsor, an organisation, a medical-representative or partner arrival source, a prescription, a sale or a per-doctor sponsor-funded value; the only arrival the vocabulary exposes is the doctor-to-doctor referral link (`person.referrer`, EARS-11). Registering a fact type is a code change; attaching a reward, a limit or a price to it is admin data.
- **EARS-33** _(realizes: US-15, US-4)_ — When a producer reports what happened through `points.record(tx, fact)` with `fact = { type, eventId, personId, ref?, occurredAt, attributes, projectId?, output?, attribution? }`, the system shall validate it against its catalog entry, evaluate it inside the producer's transaction as a savepoint (amended EARS-3) and return `{ outcome: 'applied' | 'duplicate' | 'no-effect', applied: { ruleId, ruleVersion, effect, postings }[], publishAfterCommit }`; the producer calls `publishAfterCommit()` after its own COMMIT and never after a rollback, and `record(fact)` without a transaction opens its own and publishes after its own COMMIT. The fact schema shall have **no** field for an amount, a rule id, a denomination or a dedupe key; `attribution` is written to postings only and read by no rule. A fact its catalog entry refuses (unknown type, missing or forbidden `ref`, unknown or mistyped attribute) shall throw and write nothing; a refusing debit-side effect (EARS-35) shall throw its RFC 7807 problem and write nothing, so the producer fails closed; a fact no rule applies to shall still be journaled with outcome `no-effect`, so a producer may report facts before anyone rewards them. The mobile offline sync replays through the same `record`.
- **EARS-34** _(realizes: US-11)_ — The system shall express a rule's condition as a JSON **condition AST** validated by Zod — combinators `all`, `any`, `not`; comparisons `eq`, `ne`, `in`, `gt`, `gte`, `lt`, `lte`; operands `{ attr }` (a declared attribute of the trigger fact type), `{ pred }` (a registered predicate — a read-only, side-effect-free lookup over the journal, the account or an owner's read API, e.g. `person.phoneVerified`, `person.completedLessons`, `person.referrer`, `item.price`, `item.limitedSeats`), `{ setting }` (EARS-39) and literals — with no loop, no user-defined function, no string evaluation, and a depth and node count bounded by the language. When a version is published the system shall type-check the condition against the attribute schema of **every** trigger fact type and the predicates' result types and refuse an unknown or ill-typed operand with `points-rule-parameters-invalid` naming the field path; an absent condition is `true`; at evaluation a condition reads nothing but its operands. A predicate evaluated for a fact shall see the state **including that fact**: a predicate over the journal counts the fact under evaluation as already journaled (so an invitee's first `lesson.completed` counts itself in `person.completedLessons`, EARS-11), while account and projection reads see the state before that fact's own effects.
- **EARS-35** _(realizes: US-2, US-5, US-6, US-7, US-11)_ — The system shall support exactly the **effect kinds** `credit` (issuance → beneficiary, a non-negative integer per denomination; the beneficiary is the fact's subject or the person a registered predicate returns, e.g. the inviter of EARS-11), `debit` (Pul only, person → sink, a constant or the price-list amount of the fact's ref, EARS-26), `reserve` (Pul only, the price-list amount of the fact's ref held per hold key under the item's gating mode, the advance an option of `reserve`, EARS-14…17), `settle` (the open hold → sink, EARS-19) and `release` (the open hold reversed together with any advance and its obligation, EARS-18). For one fact the system shall apply `debit`, `reserve`, `settle` and `release` before `credit`; if any `debit` or `reserve` refuses — `points-insufficient-pul`, `points-threshold-short`, `points-reserve-limit`, or `points-phone-unverified` for a person whose phone is not verified (EARS-5) — the whole fact shall be refused and nothing written, the journal row included; a `settle` or `release` with no open hold is a no-op traced `no-open-hold`; a `credit` never refuses. EARS-20 obligation netting runs as a core step after every Pul credit and is not a rule. Every `reserve`, `settle` and `release` shall, in the same transaction as its postings, write the hold projection `points_reserves` (person account, registration, item, price, the balance and advance parts, state `held` · `settled` · `released`) and, for an advance part, `points_obligations`; a reserve covered wholly by the advance writes no person-account posting, so `PointsAccount.pul.reserved` and `advance` (EARS-2) are read from these projections, which are rebuildable from the postings and the journal (the ledger `event_id` `<fact>#<rule>` names the journal row that carries the person and the registration). Obligations are kept and netted **per obligation**: EARS-20 writes one leg `obligation-settle:<obligationId>` per obligation it nets, oldest first, and `points_obligations` holds each obligation's opened, netted and remaining amounts; when the hold of an obligation is released (EARS-18) the system shall reverse that obligation's remaining advance part and return its already-netted Pul to the person by reversing exactly its own `obligation-settle` legs, so the obligation reports a remaining debt of 0, every other obligation keeps its true remaining amount, and the person ends as if that registration had never been made.
- **EARS-36** _(realizes: US-4, US-5, US-11)_ — The system shall support, as a rule's list of **limits** that must all pass, `oncePer(scope)` — scope a subset of `subject`, `beneficiary`, `ref` and declared attributes of every trigger fact type — enforced by the dedupe key `<limit key>:<scope values>` in `points_posting_keys` (amended EARS-4), the limit key the rule id unless the rule names a **shared limit key**, so that several rules grant at most one of themselves (EARS-43); `countPer(scope, period, n)` — at most `n` applications of the rule for the scope per calendar `day`, `week` or `month` in Europe/Moscow, counted from postings carrying the rule id, `n` a literal or a `{ setting }`, the count and its comparison serialised per limit scope — a transaction-scoped advisory lock keyed on (rule id, scope values, period start), taken after the person locks and in one ascending key order — because a scope need not be a locked account (EARS-11: many invitees' facts count against one inviter's cap), so concurrent facts never exceed `n`; `concurrentOpen(n, filter)` — at most `n` open holds of the person on items matching a price-list filter (EARS-21); and `unlimited`, accepted only when named explicitly. An application a limit stops is traced with that limit.
- **EARS-37** _(realizes: US-11)_ — When a rule version carries `validFrom` / `validUntil` and `active`, the system shall apply the rule only to facts whose `occurredAt` lies in [`validFrom`, `validUntil`) and only while the version active at `occurredAt` has `active: true`; deactivating a rule is publishing a new version with `active: false`, effective at once and with no release; a fact outside the window is traced `not-valid`. `effectiveFrom` (which version) and the validity window (when the rule applies at all) are independent.
- **EARS-38** _(realizes: US-11)_ — When several rules that would apply to one fact share an `exclusiveGroup`, the system shall apply only the one with the highest `priority` (a tie broken by the lexically lowest rule id) and trace the others as `superseded`; rules with no group stack, each applying on its own; the group is resolved after conditions, validity and limits, so a rule its limit stopped never suppresses a lower-priority member. When an admin publishes an active `credit` rule that shares a trigger fact type with another active `credit` rule and no exclusive group, the system shall accept it and return a stacking **warning** naming the other rule.
- **EARS-39** _(realizes: US-11)_ — The system shall keep policy parameters as versioned **settings** under the `setting.` namespace of the same immutable version table, each with its own strict schema in code — `setting.presence.share`, `setting.advance.cap`, `setting.noShow.window`, `setting.reserve.limitedSeats.max`, `setting.leaderboard.conWeight`, `setting.leaderboard.breWeight`, `setting.leaderboard.top`, `setting.leaderboard.neighbours`, `setting.effect.maxAmount` — published through the same endpoint (amended EARS-25), readable by conditions and limits as `{ setting }` operands, never triggered by a fact and never producing a posting.
- **EARS-40** _(realizes: US-12, US-13)_ — The system shall append every accepted fact to the **fact journal** `points_facts` — `event_id` (unique), type, person, ref, `occurred_at`, attributes, `received_at`, outcome and the **evaluation trace** (for each rule considered: rule id and version, trigger match, condition result, validity, each limit's verdict, the stacking verdict and the applied ledger `event_id`) — in the transaction that writes its postings; the journal shall be immutable (UPDATE and DELETE refused by a trigger, the ADR-0009 erasure re-pointing of `person_id` excepted as for postings) and carry only refs and catalog-declared attributes, never attribution.
- **EARS-41** _(realizes: US-7, US-15)_ — When a consumer asks `points.quote(personId, draftFact)`, the system shall run the same evaluation as `record` — same rules, conditions, limits, stacking and refusals — without writing, locking or publishing anything, and return the would-be outcome, the rules that would apply with their effects and postings, and the problem a refusal would raise; `record` and `quote` share one code path, so an advertised decision and the enforced one cannot diverge (EARS-13 is `quote` of a sign-up).
- **EARS-42** _(realizes: US-11, US-12)_ — When a rule version is published, activated or given a validity window, the system shall evaluate it only against facts recorded after its publication and shall never re-evaluate a journaled fact; a grant for behaviour that happened before is only the explicit, audited operation of EARS-43 or a manual adjustment (EARS-28).
- **EARS-43** _(realizes: US-2)_ — When an admin runs the **starter-bonus grant** through `POST /v1/admin/points/starter-bonus` with a mandatory reason and a `registeredBefore` instant not later than now, the system shall refuse the run with `points-starter-bonus-no-rule` (422) unless an active rule triggers on `person.starter-bonus-eligible`, and otherwise record, through `record`, the fact `person.starter-bonus-eligible` with the derived event id `starter-bonus:<personId>` for every doctor whose account was created before that instant and whose points account is not closed, so a re-run or a resumed run answers `duplicate` and never grants twice; the seeded rule `accrual.starter-bonus` [+20 Pul] credits it `oncePer(subject)` under the shared limit key `welcome` with `accrual.registration`, so a doctor receives at most one of the starter bonus and the registration grant; the run shall write, before its first grant, one `audit_ledger` record with its run id, the admin, the reason and the instant, carry that run id as the `runId` attribute of every fact it records, and write on completion one `audit_ledger` record with the counts (applied, duplicate, skipped), so a run interrupted before completion still has every grant attributed to its admin and reason. The grant is never a conversion of a legacy points balance — none exists — and publishing a rule never starts it.
- **EARS-44** _(realizes: US-11, US-12)_ — The system shall ship the PRD rules of EARS-5…11, 14…19 and 21 and the settings of EARS-39 as rows of **one new migration** (0060) that adds the engine's columns and the fact journal — the hold, obligation and price-list tables ship with the spend slice (#3810) — and inserts, at the epoch, for every seeded rule and setting id the version `max(version) + 1` of that id (1 when it has none): on a database holding only the 0059 rows that is version 2 of each 0059 accrual rule id whose meaning maps and version 1 of each new rule and setting id; nothing for the retired 0059 ids (design, «Clause map»). The seed supersedes every earlier version of its id, because the active version is the highest one in effect. It shall leave every 0058 and 0059 row, the 21 seeded version-1 rows included, and every existing posting untouched, and shall carry no refusing guard: an existing posting is immutable, cites its version by FK and is never re-evaluated (EARS-42), and the dev and stage databases hold postings and versions above 1 that earlier e2e runs wrote — a guard would fail the stage deploy every release passes. The trade-off is explicit: the seed would supersede a real admin publish just as it supersedes an e2e one — a guard on `published_by` cannot tell them apart (the e2e rows carry an admin publisher too), and skipping such an id would leave its v1-schema body, which the engine cannot parse, active. Production's protection is therefore the read-only pre-check #3850, which blocks the release that ships 0060 while any admin-published version above 1 exists there (`Release-requires: #3850`). The migration closes with a NOTICE naming every id whose seed supersedes a non-migration version, so a deploy log shows it; superseded rows stay in place, unchanged, as history. Once it is applied no PRD accrual or spend clause needs per-action engine code.
- **EARS-45** _(realizes: US-11, US-13)_ — When an admin requests `GET /v1/admin/points/fact-types` (the catalog with attribute schemas and predicates), `GET /v1/admin/points/rules` (each rule and setting id with its active version and version history) or `GET /v1/admin/points/facts?personId=…` (that person's journal rows with their evaluation traces, cursor-paginated), the system shall return them under the admin role and refuse any other caller (403).
- **EARS-46** _(realizes: US-1, US-12, US-15)_ — When a signed-in doctor requests `GET /v1/me/points/postings?cursor=…&limit=…`, the system shall return the `PostingHistory` of **their own** account only (the route takes no person parameter): every posting on the person account, newest first by (`occurred_at`, `id`), each with its posting id, `occurredAt`, denomination, a signed `amount` from the person's side (credit positive, debit negative), the fact type as `action`, the ref, `ruleId`, `ruleVersion` and `reversesPostingId` when set, cursor-paginated with a bounded page size and a stable `nextCursor`; it shall expose no attribution field and no other person's identifier, and its Pul amounts follow EARS-30.

## Event Model

### Commands

- `AccrueFromFact({ eventId, personId, action, contentRef?, projectId?, occurredAt, attribution? })` — internal; issued by the policy that consumes an accrual-source fact. Idempotent on `eventId` and on the dedupe key. _(Overridden by the production amendment: `RecordFact`.)_

### Read models

- `PointsAccount { personId, pul: { available, reserved, unit: 'Pul' }, bre, con, advance: { open: number, remaining: number }, status: 'open' | 'closed' }` — `GET /v1/me/points`; `advance.open` is the count of open advance obligations and `advance.remaining` the Pul still owed across them; Pul available, Bre and Con are a fold over the person account's postings, and `pul.reserved`, `advance.open` and `advance.remaining` are read from the hold projections `points_reserves` and `points_obligations` (EARS-2, EARS-35) — never a mutable balance column (a cache, if ever added, is derived and rebuildable).
- `PostingHistory { items: { postingId, occurredAt, denomination, amount, action, contentRef?, ruleId, ruleVersion, reversesPostingId? }[], nextCursor }` — `GET /v1/me/points/postings`.
- `GatingDecision { itemRef, mode, pulCost, decision: 'open' | 'reserve' | 'reserve-with-advance' | 'short' | 'blocked', reason?: 'points-phone-unverified' | 'points-reserve-limit' | 'points-account-closed', reservedFromBalance, coveredByAdvance, shortfall, earnHint?: { pulToEarn, lessonsNeeded } }` — `GET /v1/me/points/gating/:itemRef`; `reason` is present on `blocked` only.
- `MonthlyLeaderboard { specialtyCode, month, top: Row[10], own?: { rank, score, neighbours: Row[] } }` with `Row { rank, displayName, city?, photoRef?, score }` — `GET /v1/points/leaderboard/:specialtyCode?month=YYYY-MM`.
- `AttentionAggregate { projectId, outputType?, outputId?, month, denomination, ruleId, ruleVersion, amount, persons }` — `GET /v1/admin/points/aggregates` (service/admin scope, consumed by 036).
- `FactJournalEntry { eventId, type, ref?, occurredAt, receivedAt, outcome, trace }` — `GET /v1/admin/points/facts` (EARS-45).

### Policies

- An accrual-source fact becomes postings only through the active rule version at the fact's `occurredAt`; a re-tuned rule never rewrites past postings.
- A posting that would take a person's available Pul below zero is refused; reserves and advances, not debts, cover a price.
- A sponsor, medical-representative or partner arrival source is written to the posting's attribution fields and read by no rule.
- A closed account accepts no new posting.

## EARS requirements

> Flat numbering per ADR-0006 §4. Every clause carries `realizes: US-N` against [`025-product.md`](./025-product.md) and is tagged in [`025-scenarios.feature`](./025-scenarios.feature). Defaults in brackets are rule, setting or price-list parameters (EARS-25, EARS-39), never literals. EARS-3, EARS-4 and EARS-25 below are the running-production baseline, overridden by the production amendment above; EARS-32…46 are declared there. Where a clause below names a **seeded rule**, that rule is admin data shipped by EARS-44 and its trigger, condition, effect and limits are the ones stated; changing it is a new rule version, never engine code.

- **EARS-1** _(realizes: US-1, US-15)_ — When the engine first needs a person's attention-points account (the first accrual fact for that person), the system shall open **exactly one** ledger account with `subject_kind = person`, `unit = attention_points` for that person, unique on (`subject_id`, `unit`), and every storefront, the Academy, the mobile app and every consumer feature shall resolve that same account through the one API; no per-storefront or per-device account shall exist. A read never opens an account: a read for a person with no account yet answers the zero fold of an open account.
- **EARS-2** _(realizes: US-1, US-15)_ — When a signed-in doctor requests `GET /v1/me/points`, the system shall return the `PointsAccount` read model whose Pul available, Bre and Con are a **fold over the person account's postings** and whose Pul reserved and open advance with its remaining obligation are read from the hold projections `points_reserves` and `points_obligations` (EARS-35) — never a mutable balance column read or written anywhere — and the same request from any client shall return the same values; a request for another person's account shall be refused (403).
- **EARS-3** _(realizes: US-2, US-3, US-12, US-15)_ — When an accrual-source fact listed in the Event Model is accepted, the system shall evaluate the rule version **active at the fact's `occurredAt`** for that action and write one immutable double-entry posting per non-zero denomination (from the system issuance account to the person account) after inserting the fact's one `points_posting_keys` row (its `event_id` and its dedupe key), each posting carrying the fact's `event_id`, a `leg` naming the posting within the fact (denomination + entry kind), the denomination, the `rule_id` and `rule_version`, the project and output when the fact names them, the attribution subject when one is recorded, `occurred_at` and `recorded_at`, all in one transaction, and shall then emit `PointsCredited` — the only event from which a consumer may state a credited amount.
- **EARS-4** _(realizes: US-4, US-1)_ — When an accrual-source fact arrives whose `event_id` or whose dedupe key **(person, content item, action)** already has a `points_posting_keys` row — whether it came from another storefront, the Academy, a retry, or the mobile app's offline sync — the system shall write **no** posting and shall answer with the original outcome (`duplicate`, referencing the existing postings), so a replayed or concurrent fact never accrues twice; the uniqueness shall be enforced by database constraints, not only by a read-before-write.
- **EARS-5** _(realizes: US-2)_ — When `account.registered` or `phone.verified` is recorded for a doctor and both hold (in either order — the condition reads `person.registered` and `person.phoneVerified`), the seeded rule `accrual.registration` shall credit the **registration grant** [+20 Pul] `oncePer(subject)` under the shared limit key `welcome` (EARS-43); no Pul shall be spendable by a person whose phone is not verified — every `debit` and `reserve` refuses with `points-phone-unverified` (EARS-35).
- **EARS-6** _(realizes: US-2, US-3)_ — When feature 022 records `profile.verified` for a doctor, the seeded rule `accrual.profile` shall credit the **profile grant** [+30 Pul, +2 Bre] `oncePer(subject)`.
- **EARS-7** _(realizes: US-2, US-3, US-4)_ — When feature 024 records `lesson.completed` — its computed completion status, which already requires watch time or a passed test — the seeded rules shall credit the **lesson award** [+10 Pul, +1 Bre] as two parts: `accrual.lesson` credits [+10 Pul] when the fact's `mode` is online, `oncePer(subject, ref)` under the limit key `lesson.pul`, and `accrual.lesson.any-mode` credits [+1 Bre] for any `mode` (online, offline or open learning), `oncePer(subject, ref)` under the limit key `lesson`; so a lesson's Bre is credited once whatever its mode, an offline or open completion earns no Pul, and an online completion after an offline one still earns the Pul but no second Bre (design, «Clause map», mode order); a test mistake, a failed attempt or a repeated pass shall never produce a debiting posting; a podcast completed outside its lesson is no fact type and accrues nothing.
- **EARS-8** _(realizes: US-2, US-3)_ — When feature 024 records `module.completed` (all lessons done and the module test passed), the seeded rules shall credit the **module award** [+20 Pul, +3 Bre, +1 Con] as two parts: `accrual.module` [+20 Pul] for an online `mode` under the limit key `module.pul`, and `accrual.module.any-mode` [+3 Bre, +1 Con] for any `mode` under the limit key `module`, both `oncePer(subject, ref)`, with the same mode order as EARS-7.
- **EARS-9** _(realizes: US-3, US-5)_ — When `attendance.confirmed` is recorded with a server-side `presenceSharePercent` of at least `setting.presence.share` [30 % of the item's duration], the seeded rule `accrual.participation` shall credit the **participation award** [+2 Bre, +1 Con] `oncePer(subject, ref)`, identical at every price and for open items; the catalog marks `attendance.confirmed` and the engagement facts of EARS-10 as not earning Pul, so no rule on them can credit Pul (amended EARS-25); a presence share below the setting shall not count as attendance. The room is the producer: when the event reaches `ended` (007 `CloseRoom`, the only edge into `ended`), inside the transaction that commits the end, it records one `attendance.confirmed` per doctor with presence on the event — `ref` = the event, `presenceSharePercent` = ⌊100 × the doctor's 006 EARS-5 presence minutes ÷ the event's **scheduled** duration (`duration_min`)⌋ clamped to 0…100, `registrationId` = the doctor's active registration or null, `occurredAt` = the end, event id `attendance.confirmed:<person>:<event>` — and records shares below the setting too, so the rule's condition, not the producer, decides; `PointsCredited` is published after that COMMIT only.
- **EARS-10** _(realizes: US-3, US-4)_ — When a doctor's question to the speaker, item rating or interactive answer during a live event is recorded (`event.question-asked`, `event.rated`, `event.interactive-answered`), the seeded rules `accrual.question`, `accrual.rating` and `accrual.interactive` shall each credit its **engagement award** [+1 Bre] at most `oncePer(subject, ref)`; a further report of the same action on the same item shall answer `duplicate` (amended EARS-4). The producers are pending: the platform has no server-side question-to-the-speaker, poll answer or rating action yet — they are 020 wave-2 work (020 EARS-13, EARS-16) — and #3879 wires each fact on its action's server path once it exists; until then EARS-10 is unexercisable, not stubbed.
- **EARS-11** _(realizes: US-2, US-3, US-8)_ — When `phone.verified` or `lesson.completed` is recorded for an invitee whose `person.referrer` is set (the link 021 recorded with `referral.attributed`) and who has both a verified phone and at least one completed lesson, the seeded rule `accrual.referral-inviter` shall credit the inviter — the beneficiary `person.referrer` — [+30 Pul, +2 Bre] with the limits `oncePer(beneficiary, subject)` and `countPer(beneficiary, month, 5)` (the **monthly referral cap**, Europe/Moscow), and the seeded rule `accrual.referral-invitee` shall credit the invitee [+30 Pul] `oncePer(subject)`; when the inviter's cap is reached only the inviter's application is skipped (`limit-reached`), the invitee's still applies.
- **EARS-12** _(realizes: US-4, US-3)_ — The system shall write **no posting that debits Bre or Con** from a person account other than a compensating posting that reverses a specific earlier accrual (EARS-28); no rule shall express a penalty, and no posting shall convert between denominations (1 Con = 2 Bre is a read-side weight only); a manual adjustment (EARS-28) may be positive in any denomination and negative in Pul only.
- **EARS-13** _(realizes: US-7, US-15)_ — When a signed-in doctor requests `GET /v1/me/points/gating/:itemRef`, the system shall return the `GatingDecision` for that doctor, computed as `quote` (EARS-41) of the draft fact `registration.created` on that item: `open` for an item with no price-list row, an `open` row or a zero price, and for any lesson or module; for a priced item, `reserve` when the available Pul covers the price, `reserve-with-advance` in advance mode when the shortfall fits the advance cap (EARS-16), and otherwise `short` with the exact **shortfall** (price − available Pul) and an `earnHint` whose `pulToEarn` is the shortfall **plus the remaining open obligations** — because EARS-20 nets every new Pul accrual to the obligations first — and whose `lessonsNeeded` is `ceil(pulToEarn / the Pul credit of the active accrual.lesson version)`; never a negative number and never a «not enough» decision in advance mode within the cap. The read is a read: it answers every valid request 200 with a decision, never with a state problem — when the sign-up's `record` would refuse for the doctor's account state rather than the price, the decision is `blocked` with `reason` = that problem's slug (`points-phone-unverified` EARS-5, `points-reserve-limit` EARS-21, `points-account-closed` EARS-31). Only a malformed `itemRef` (not an event id or `<kind>:<id>` of letters, digits and `:._-`) is refused, 400 `points-item-ref-invalid`; 401 / 403 as for EARS-2. Until the advance slice (#3811) an advance-mode item is decided like a threshold one (`reserve` or `short`, never `reserve-with-advance`).
- **EARS-14** _(realizes: US-5)_ — When `registration.created` is recorded for an item whose price-list row has a Pul price > 0 and the doctor's available Pul covers it, the seeded rule `spend.reserve` (`oncePer(subject, registrationId)`) shall **reserve** the price by a posting from the person account to the system reserve account referencing the registration and the item; an open item or a zero price shall produce no posting (outcome `no-effect`).
- **EARS-15** _(realizes: US-6, US-8)_ — When `registration.created` is recorded for an **advance-mode** item and the available Pul is below the price, the `reserve` effect shall reserve the available Pul from the balance and cover **exactly the shortfall** by an advance posting from the system advance account, opening an obligation of that amount for the person; the decision shall be the same for every doctor on that item and shall not read the arrival source.
- **EARS-16** _(realizes: US-6)_ — If opening an advance would take the person's open obligations above `setting.advance.cap` [150 Pul, counted per person], then the `reserve` effect shall treat the item as **threshold** for that doctor (EARS-17); an open obligation shall never block a reserve-free action — open items and lessons stay open — and shall never be presented by the API as a negative balance.
- **EARS-17** _(realizes: US-7)_ — When `registration.created` is recorded for a **threshold-mode** item (or an advance-mode item beyond the cap) and the available Pul is below the price, the `reserve` effect shall refuse the fact with RFC 7807 problem `points-threshold-short` carrying the shortfall and the `earnHint` of EARS-13, write nothing, and leave the sign-up to fail closed in its producer. Until the advance slice (#3811) every advance-mode item short of Pul is refused this way, as if beyond the cap.
- **EARS-18** _(realizes: US-5, US-6)_ — When a held registration is cancelled by the doctor at any time (`registration.cancelled`), voided because the platform cancelled or rescheduled the item (`registration.voided`), or the item ends without `attendance.confirmed` for that registration within `setting.noShow.window` [24 hours after the item's end] — the no-show sweep then records `registration.no-show`, idempotent through the derived event id `no-show:<registrationId>` — the seeded rule `spend.release` shall **release** the hold by a compensating posting (`reverses_posting_id` → the reserve) and annul an advance used for that item together with its obligation by a compensating posting; on a no-show no participation award shall be accrued; no other consequence shall follow. An `attendance.confirmed` recorded after the no-show release shall settle nothing (`no-open-hold`, the released reserve stays released) and shall still credit the participation award of EARS-9, traced as late. Producers: the no-show sweep is 025's own (wired, #3810); `registration.cancelled` and `registration.voided` have no server-side origin yet — 005 has no cancellation in wave 1 (#2040) and the events lifecycle no cancelled-or-moved state (020 EARS-10, #1773) — so those two release paths are pending their producers (#3884), not stubbed.
- **EARS-19** _(realizes: US-5)_ — When `attendance.confirmed` is recorded for a held registration with a presence share of at least `setting.presence.share` (EARS-9), the seeded rule `spend.settle` (`oncePer(subject, registrationId)`) shall **debit** the hold by a posting from the system reserve account to the system sink account referencing the reserve, exactly once per registration.
- **EARS-20** _(realizes: US-6)_ — When Pul is accrued to a person with an open obligation, the system shall first **settle the obligation** by a posting from the person account to the system advance account of `min(accrued Pul, remaining obligation)` in the same transaction, oldest obligation first, and emit `AdvanceSettled` with the remaining amount.
- **EARS-21** _(realizes: US-5)_ — If a reserve would give a person more open holds on limited-seat items than `setting.reserve.limitedSeats.max` [3] — the `concurrentOpen` limit of `spend.reserve`, filtered to price-list rows with `limitedSeats` — then the system shall refuse the fact with problem `points-reserve-limit` and write nothing; the waiting list stays with 020.
- **EARS-22** _(realizes: US-5, US-4)_ — The system shall refuse any posting that would take a person's **available Pul below zero**, serialising postings per person account so concurrent reserves cannot overdraw it, and shall write no time-based expiry posting — Pul does not expire.

- **EARS-26** _(realizes: US-11)_ — When an admin sets an item's price-list row through `PUT /v1/admin/points/prices/:itemRef` with its item kind (a kind registered in the catalog — `event`, `course`, `training`, `internship`, `offline-event`, `lesson`, `module`, …), mode `open`, `advance` or `threshold`, a Pul price [standard 50, premium or limited-seat 100] and the `limitedSeats` flag, the system shall accept any mode for a kind the catalog marks priceable, refuse any mode but `open` for a lesson or a module and for an НМО-accredited event (problem `points-gating-invalid`), audit the change, and apply it to reserves and debits created afterwards; existing holds keep the price they were reserved at. ## Invariants

- One person has at most one open attention-points account.
- The balance of every denomination equals the fold of its postings; no stored balance exists.
- No posting row is ever updated or deleted; every posting is unique on (`event_id`, `leg`) with a non-null rule id and version, and every fact has exactly one `points_posting_keys` row. _(Overridden by the production amendment, amended EARS-3/EARS-4: every applied rule — the ledger fact `<fact event id>#<rule id>` — has exactly one `points_posting_keys` row, so a journal fact has 0…n key rows.)_
- A (person, content item, action) dedupe key has at most one accrual posting set. _(Overridden by the production amendment: an applied rule's `oncePer` key `<limit key>:<scope values>` has at most one posting set, and a fact `event_id` has at most one journal row.)_
- Available Pul is never below zero; Bre and Con decrease only by a reversal of a specific accrual.
- No event accrues Pul; no lesson or module costs Pul; an НМО-accredited event is always open.
- A sponsor, medical-representative or partner arrival source never changes a gating decision, a price or an accrual.
- Open obligations of a person never exceed the active advance cap.
- A leaderboard response contains only doctors with an active dissemination consent, ranked among themselves.
- An attention aggregate contains no person identifier.
- No rule reads anything but catalog-declared attributes, registered predicates and settings; no fact type or predicate exposes a sponsor, an arrival source other than the referral link, a prescription or a sale.
- A journaled fact is never re-evaluated; a rule version applies only to facts recorded after its publication.
- `record` and `quote` share one evaluation path.

## Open questions carried forward

For the Product Lead, designed around rather than resolved here:

- **Con↔Bre exchange visible to the doctor** — default: a weight only (EARS-12, EARS-23); a doctor-facing exchange would be a new posting kind, not a schema change.
- **Sponsor billing weights** of Con, Bre and Pul (036) — every posting carries its rule version (EARS-27), so any weighting can be recomputed from EARS-29.
- **Lesson completed offline first, online later** (EARS-7, EARS-8) — default: the Bre (and a module's Con) is credited once whatever the mode, and the later online completion still earns the Pul, so the doctor ends with the full award [+10 Pul, +1 Bre] and never a second Bre; the alternative — the offline completion forfeits that lesson's Pul for good — is reached by publishing new versions of the two rules without sharing a dedupe key: the Pul rule gains a registered-predicate condition that it applies only when no prior completion of that lesson exists (offline or online), while the Bre rule keeps its own key — putting `accrual.lesson` back under the shared key `lesson` would instead collide the two rules on every online completion and lose the Pul or the Bre.
- **Starter-bonus amount** — default [+20 Pul], the registration grant's (EARS-43); the amount is a rule version the Product Lead sets before the run.
- **Pul prices on courses and trainings** — the price list accepts any priceable kind (EARS-26); the PRD's «lessons never cost Pul» keeps lessons and modules open, so a course whose access is priced must not price its own lessons — the kind is registered priceable only when its owner's spec says so.
