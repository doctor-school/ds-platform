# 030 — Projects: the container, its admin, the «Все проекты» catalog and the project page (Requirements, slice 1)

## Outcomes

- A project is a container, not a type: it declares the results it produces — each of a kind from a list the platform administrators maintain — and no surface labels a project as a «Школа» or a «Медиа».
- A project has a public stage — running, «Скоро» (in preparation, with a line on what is happening now) or «Завершён» — separate from its draft / published / retired life.
- Platform administrators describe a project once in the admin — fields, stage, results, organisations from the 032 registry, display order — and the catalog card, the Academy home block and the project page follow it with no page editing.
- `academy.doctor.school/projects` lists the public projects in the operator's order under a scale line computed from data; one card design serves the catalog and the Academy home.
- Every card opens a project page with the hero, the results, the organisations and the «Войти в проект» entries to 033 and 034; nothing on the page links to a page that does not exist.
- The feature-012 project records stay projects with every link kept; their old kind is never turned into a result by a guess.

## Scope

- The admin «Проекты» form (fields, stage, results editor), the reshaped «Организации» panel of a project, the «Порядок показа» screen and the «Виды результатов» section in `apps/admin`, built from the approved admin kit of `@ds/design-system`.
- The project, result, output-kind, display-order and project-organisation endpoints in `apps/api` (the `taxonomy` module, where projects live), their Zod contract in `packages/schemas`, and the Drizzle schema and forward migration in `packages/db`.
- The public catalog, scale-line, project and project-results reads in `apps/api`.
- The shared project card block in `@ds/design-system`; the Academy routes `/projects` and `/projects/{slug}` in `apps/portal`, the «Проекты» item of the Academy navigation and the Academy home projects block.
- The forward migration that absorbs the 012 project records into this model.

### Out of scope

- The project dashboard (market size, audience, investment, potential) and its admin entry, and the canvas «первоинвестор» wording of the investor entry — late slice of 030, `blocked_by` 036.
- The team block (experts with their role in the project, the «Место открыто» card) and every link to an expert page — later slice of 030, after 031.
- The cross-cutting project number (REQ-89) — 036.
- The «Закулисье» backstage block — later slice of 030, after the materials feed 048 can attribute materials to a project.
- The investor's annual exclusive contract as a public fact on the page («Годовой эксклюзивный контракт», «Период») — the late slice of 032, `blocked_by` 036.
- Outputs as content (school → course → module → lesson, playable podcasts and clubs) — 023 / 024; slice 1 declares results and links to where they already live.
- Visitor filters and sorting in the catalog (REQ-138); event-level partners (REQ-140).
- The organisation registry itself — 032 slice 1; the application pages the entries lead to — 033 / 034 slice 1.
- The Academy home re-layout (hero, persona blocks, placement of the projects block) — 029; slice 1 replaces only the data and the card of the existing home projects section.
- The «Эксперты» navigation item and the expert pages — 031.

## Constraints

- `projects` is the absorbed 012 row family (ADR-0016 §9): id, slug, `first_published_at`, status, `deleted_at`, version, cover and every `event_projects`, `project_experts` and `project_organisations` row survive; no row is re-created.
- The output-kind list is data, not code: adding, renaming or retiring a kind is a row change, never a migration or a deploy (032 role-list precedent).
- Organisations in a project come only from the 032 registry; every public projection of an organisation follows 032 EARS-5.
- A result's storefront link is an absolute HTTPS URL on the configured doctor-storefront origin; no other host is accepted.
- The doctor storefront (`apps/doctor`) serves no projects catalog or project page and reads no project contract (ADR-0016 §8; REQ-51).
- Slugs stay system-generated and permanent after the first publish (012 authoring contract).

## Event Model

**Commands** — `CreateProject`, `UpdateProject` (fields, stage, stage note), `ReplaceProjectResults`, `ReorderProjects`, `PublishProject`, `RetireProject`, `RestoreProject`; `LinkProjectOrganisation`, `UpdateProjectOrganisation`, `RetireProjectOrganisation`, `RestoreProjectOrganisation`; `CreateOutputKind`, `RenameOutputKind`, `RetireOutputKind`, `RestoreOutputKind` (platform administrator).

**Events** — `ProjectCreated`, `ProjectUpdated`, `ProjectResultsReplaced`, `ProjectsReordered`, `ProjectPublished`, `ProjectRetired`, `ProjectRestored`; `ProjectOrganisationLinked`, `ProjectOrganisationUpdated`, `ProjectOrganisationRetired`, `ProjectOrganisationRestored`; `OutputKindCreated`, `OutputKindRenamed`, `OutputKindRetired`, `OutputKindRestored` — each a 010 change-audit row under the request's actor.

**Read models** — the admin project list, detail and display order; the output-kind list with the number of results of each kind; the public catalog (ordered cards); the public scale line; the public project; the public project results.

**Policies** — «a project has no kind; its outputs are its declared results»; «an output kind is a row of the list, never a constant»; «a public project is published and declares at least one result»; «running and «Скоро» before «Завершён», each in the operator's order»; «the home shows the first three of the catalog»; «an organisation reaches a project only from the registry and only through its display rules»; «restore returns to draft»; «no public link points at a page that does not exist».

## EARS requirements

> Flat numbering per ADR-0006 §4. Every clause realizes one or more PRD stories and is covered by `030-scenarios.feature`.

- **EARS-1** _(realizes: US-11, US-3)_ — WHEN a platform administrator creates or edits a project on the «Проекты» full-page form, THE SYSTEM SHALL persist the retained `projects` row with the title (trimmed 1–160), the short description (trimmed 1–2000, nullable only on a draft), the optional normalized cover of the 012 media contract, the stage — one of «Проект идёт» (`running`), «Скоро» (`soon`, the default of a new project) or «Завершён» (`completed`) — and the stage note «Что происходит сейчас» (trimmed 1–80), which SHALL be required while the stage is `soon` and stored empty otherwise; the form SHALL offer no project kind; a refused write SHALL show its field-addressed reasons on the form and change nothing.
- **EARS-2** _(realizes: US-2, US-8, US-11)_ — WHEN a platform administrator saves a project's results on the «Результаты» tab, THE SYSTEM SHALL store them as an ordered list of 0–20 results, each with an output kind, a title (trimmed 1–120), an optional description (trimmed 0–300) and an optional storefront link, replaced as one set under the project's `If-Match` version; THE SYSTEM SHALL refuse a storefront link that is not an absolute HTTPS URL on the configured doctor-storefront origin, and SHALL refuse an output kind that is unknown or retired unless the result already held it, keeping such a result shown marked «Вид снят».
- **EARS-3** _(realizes: US-2, US-11)_ — WHEN a platform administrator opens «Виды результатов», THE SYSTEM SHALL list every output kind with its title, its status and the number of results of that kind, on the `AdminDataList` composition; WHEN the administrator adds or renames a kind, THE SYSTEM SHALL persist one retained `project_output_kinds` row with a stable id, a system-generated slug, a version and a title (trimmed 1–60) unique among active kinds case-insensitively, and SHALL offer it on every results editor at once; WHEN the administrator retires a kind that a result of a draft or published project holds, THE SYSTEM SHALL refuse with `409 OUTPUT_KIND_IN_USE` listing those projects, otherwise SHALL retire it and stop offering it, and SHALL restore a retired kind as active under the same id. The list SHALL be seeded with the active kinds «Школа», «Курс», «Подкаст», «Событие», «Конгресс», «Клуб».
- **EARS-4** _(realizes: US-9, US-11)_ — WHEN a platform administrator links an organisation to a project in the project's «Организации» panel, THE SYSTEM SHALL offer only non-retired organisations of the 032 registry, SHALL store on the `project_organisations` row the role the organisation plays in the project — one of the active roles that organisation holds — and an optional note (trimmed 0–160), and SHALL let at most one active link be marked «Инвестор проекта»; THE SYSTEM SHALL refuse an organisation that is retired or unknown and a role the organisation does not hold, and the link's retire and restore SHALL follow 012 EARS-13 / EARS-14.
- **EARS-5** _(realizes: US-12)_ — WHEN a platform administrator moves a project on the «Порядок показа» screen and saves, THE SYSTEM SHALL store the new order of every non-retired project as one write guarded by the order's current version, SHALL refuse a stale order with `412 PRECONDITION_FAILED` and change nothing, and SHALL place a newly created project last; the catalog, the Academy home block and the scale line SHALL follow the next read.
- **EARS-6** _(realizes: US-14)_ — WHEN a platform administrator publishes a draft project («Опубликовать»), THE SYSTEM SHALL refuse with `409 PUBLISH_REQUIREMENTS_NOT_MET` and field errors naming each missing item — the title, the description, at least one result, the stage note of a `soon` project, and the single active curator of 012 EARS-5 — and otherwise SHALL set `first_published_at` once and transition the project to `published`; an edit of a published project that would leave any of these missing SHALL be refused with the same code.
- **EARS-7** _(realizes: US-13)_ — WHEN a platform administrator retires a project («Снять с публикации») through the signed lifecycle-impact preview of 012 EARS-13, THE SYSTEM SHALL set it `retired`, keep every result, event, expert and organisation link, and remove it at once from the catalog, the Academy home block, the scale line and the public project reads; WHEN the administrator restores it («Вернуть»), THE SYSTEM SHALL return it to `draft` through 012 EARS-14 with its fields, results and links, and it SHALL reappear publicly only after a later publish.
- **EARS-8** _(realizes: US-1, US-2, US-3, US-4)_ — WHEN any caller reads `GET /v1/public/projects`, THE SYSTEM SHALL return every public project — published and declaring at least one result — first the `running` and `soon` projects and then the `completed` ones, each group in the operator's order, each item carrying the slug, title, description, cover URL or `null`, stage, stage note, the outputs — the titles of the distinct kinds of its results in result order — and the investor `{ title, logoUrl }` of its «Инвестор проекта» link or `null`, the investor projected by 032 EARS-5 (omitted when its name rule is off or it is not published, `logoUrl: null` when its logo rule is off); the item SHALL carry no project kind, and the project summaries embedded in the existing relationship reads SHALL drop `kind` and carry the stage.
- **EARS-9** _(realizes: US-5)_ — WHEN any caller reads `GET /v1/public/projects/scale`, THE SYSTEM SHALL return `{ projects, partners, broadcasts, firstBroadcastYear }` computed at the moment of the request: the number of public projects, the published-organisation count of 032 EARS-12, the number of distinct events in a public state (`published`, `live`, `ended`, `in_archive`) linked by an active `event_projects` row to a public project, and the year of the earliest start among those events or `null`; no figure SHALL be stored or entered by hand.
- **EARS-10** _(realizes: US-1, US-5, US-17)_ — WHEN a visitor, signed in or not, opens `academy.doctor.school/projects` — from the «Проекты» item of the Academy navigation or from «Все проекты →» — THE SYSTEM SHALL render the header with the scale line «N проектов · M партнёров» and, when `broadcasts` is above zero, «K эфиров с <год> года» in Russian plural forms, the grid of project cards of EARS-8 in that order, and the closing invitation whose actions lead to the 033 application page and the 034 investor page; WHEN there is no public project, THE SYSTEM SHALL say that projects are being prepared, show no grid and no scale line, and keep the closing invitation; WHEN the read fails, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the error state with «Повторить».
- **EARS-11** _(realizes: US-2, US-3, US-4, US-7)_ — WHEN the shared project card renders a catalog item, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the cover or, without one, the two-letter emblem of the title; the kicker «Проект» with the badge «Скоро» for a `soon` project or «Завершён» for a `completed` one and no badge for a `running` one; the title; the description; the outputs line («школа · конгресс · клуб») or, for a `soon` project, its stage note; and «Партнёр · <название>» when the item carries an investor; the whole card SHALL be one keyboard-reachable link to `/projects/{slug}`.
- **EARS-12** _(realizes: US-6)_ — WHEN a visitor opens the Academy home, THE SYSTEM SHALL render in its projects block the first three items of the EARS-8 read with the same card and «Все проекты →» linking to `/projects`, and SHALL hide the block when there is no public project; publishing, retiring or reordering a project SHALL change the home, the catalog and the project page on their next read.
- **EARS-13** _(realizes: US-7, US-8, US-9)_ — WHEN any caller reads `GET /v1/public/projects/{idOrSlug}`, THE SYSTEM SHALL answer a public project with its slug, title, description, cover URL, stage, stage note, the curator's display name and nothing else of the curator, the counts of its results and of its active published experts, the since-year — the year of its earliest public event start, else of its `first_published_at` — its organisations projected by 032 EARS-5 (the investor and the other links with their role title and note) and `investorPlaceOpen`, true only when the project has no active «Инвестор проекта» link; and SHALL answer `GET /v1/public/projects/{idOrSlug}/results` with its results in order (kind title, title, description, storefront link); a draft, retired, non-public or unknown project SHALL be answered `404` alike.
- **EARS-14** _(realizes: US-7, US-8, US-17)_ — WHEN a visitor opens `academy.doctor.school/projects/{slug}` of a public project, THE SYSTEM SHALL render the breadcrumb «Проекты / <название>» back to the catalog; the hero with the kicker «Проект», the stage badge («проект идёт»; «Скоро» with the stage note; «Завершён»), the title, the description, «Куратор проекта — <имя>» as plain text with no link, and «N результатов · M экспертов · с <год> года»; and the «Результаты» block of cards — kind, title, description and «на doctor.school ↗» only when the result has a storefront link, every result of a `soon` project marked «в плане» with no link — with its own loading state and its load-error state «Не удалось загрузить результаты проекта» with «Повторить» that leaves the rest of the page working; WHEN the project is not public, THE SYSTEM SHALL render the Academy not-found page.
- **EARS-15** _(realizes: US-4, US-9)_ — WHEN the project page renders its «Организации в проекте» block, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the investor under «Инвестор проекта» with its logo or, when `logoUrl` is `null`, its name as text with «Логотип не размещается — по комплаенсу партнёра организация названа текстом.», and every other organisation under its role title with its note; WHEN `investorPlaceOpen` is true, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the free place «Стать инвестором проекта» with «Инвесторам →» leading to the 034 investor page; an investor hidden by its name rule SHALL show neither its card nor the free place.
- **EARS-16** _(realizes: US-10)_ — WHEN the project page renders «Войти в проект», THE SYSTEM SHALL show the expert card «Место в команде открыто» with «Зайти как эксперт — подать заявку» leading to the 033 application page and the investor card «Обсудить участие в проекте» with «Обсудить проект» leading to the 034 investor page; the page SHALL show no dashboard, no project number, no team block, no «Закулисье» block and no link to an expert page.
- **EARS-17** _(realizes: US-11, US-13, US-14)_ — WHEN the forward migration of this feature runs, THE SYSTEM SHALL keep every `projects` row with its id, slug, status, `first_published_at`, version, cover, description and every event, expert and organisation link, SHALL set each row's stage to `running` with no results, SHALL keep its former 012 kind only as the read-only `legacy_kind` the admin form shows as «Прежний тип (012): <Школа | Медиа | Программа> — укажите результаты проекта», and SHALL give every existing `project_organisations` link the investor role of 032; no result SHALL be created from the former kind, and a published project without results SHALL stay off every public read (EARS-8) while the admin list marks it «Не на сайте: нет результатов».
- **EARS-18** _(realizes: US-15)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL serve the «Проекты», «Порядок показа» and «Виды результатов» sections, the project «Организации» panel and every endpoint behind them only to a `platform_admin` in the dedicated admin session with the second factor (011, 047), with CSRF, and SHALL refuse anyone else server-side; every mutating endpoint SHALL require a canonical UUID idempotency key and, except for create, the current `If-Match` version per 012 EARS-17, and every change to a project, its results, its order, its organisation links or an output kind SHALL be recorded in the 010 change audit under the acting administrator.
- **EARS-19** _(realizes: US-16)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL serve the projects catalog and the project page only on the Academy host: the doctor storefront SHALL have no `/projects` route and SHALL import no project contract, and doctor-storefront requests for those paths SHALL get its not-found page.
- **EARS-20** _(realizes: US-17)_ — WHILE the viewport is a phone or a narrow screen, THE SYSTEM SHALL keep the catalog, the card grid, the project page, the admin project form with its results editor, the «Порядок показа» screen and the «Виды результатов» section usable without horizontal scrolling of the page, with every card and action keyboard-reachable, and every one of these screens SHALL pass the platform's `playwright-axe` gate.

- **EARS-21** _(realizes: US-10, US-11)_ — WHEN a platform administrator sets or clears the optional «Ищем эксперта по специальности» field of a project on the «Проекты» form, THE SYSTEM SHALL store on the `projects` row a nullable reference to one entry of the Минздрав specialty book `specialties_minzdrav` (`packages/db/src/schema/specialties.ts`, the book the 012 taxonomy links through `direction_specialties`) and SHALL refuse an unknown entry with a field error and no change; `GET /v1/public/projects/{idOrSlug}` SHALL carry it as `soughtSpecialty { code, name }` or `null`; WHEN the project page renders the expert card of «Войти в проект» and `soughtSpecialty` is set, THE SYSTEM SHALL show «Проект ищет эксперта по специальности «<name>».» with the entry's verbatim name, and WHEN it is `null`, THE SYSTEM SHALL show no sentence about a specialty while keeping the card's heading and its action.

## Invariants

- No project carries a kind on any public read or form; its outputs are its declared results (EARS-1, EARS-8, EARS-17).
- A public project is published and declares at least one result; only public projects appear in the catalog, on the home, in the scale line and on a project page (EARS-6, EARS-8, EARS-9, EARS-13).
- The home block is the first three items of the catalog read — one source, one card (EARS-11, EARS-12).
- No organisation reaches a project except from the 032 registry, and no public projection breaks 032 EARS-5 (EARS-4, EARS-8, EARS-15).
- A result's storefront link points only at the doctor-storefront origin; a result without one shows no link (EARS-2, EARS-14).
- No public link of the catalog or the page points at an expert page, a dashboard or any page not yet delivered (EARS-10, EARS-14, EARS-16).
- Restore never publishes (EARS-7).
- Every project write is made by a `platform_admin` with the second factor and leaves one 010 audit row (EARS-18).
