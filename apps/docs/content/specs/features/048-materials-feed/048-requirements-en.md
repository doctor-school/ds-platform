# 048 — Materials feed: own materials, the reading page, the rail on the Academy home and the Telegram and VK connectors (Requirements, slices 1–3)

## Outcomes

- A platform administrator writes a news item or an expert column in the admin, previews it, publishes it, and it is the first card of the rail on the Academy home with no release and no page editing.
- An edit or an unpublish of a material shows on the site on the next read; an unpublished material is on no public surface.
- The Academy home carries one horizontal rail of materials, newest first, that a visitor moves with ←/→, a swipe or the keyboard, that loads older materials near its end and never moves or plays by itself.
- Every own material has its reading page on `academy.doctor.school`; every card leads to a page that exists.
- The feed is one store of entries in one card shape, so each later network slice adds a connector and changes nothing built here (PRD US-10).

## Scope

- The «Материалы» section of `apps/admin`: list, full-page form with the rich-text editor, preview, publish and unpublish, built from the approved admin kit of `@ds/design-system` plus its new rich-text editor primitive.
- The material and feed-entry tables in `packages/db` with their forward migration; the admin material endpoints, the public feed read and the public material read in `apps/api`; their Zod contract, including the rich-text body document, in `packages/schemas`.
- The shared feed card, the rail and the rich-text renderer in `@ds/design-system`; the reading page route `/materials/{slug}` and the rail section of the Academy home in `apps/portal`.

### Out of scope

- The admin source list (US-3), automatic intake from networks (US-4), hiding one external material (US-5) and external cards opening on their platform (US-7) — slices 2–5 of 048, one network each (Telegram, VK / VK Видео, RuTube, Dzen).
- Attributing a material to a project — the later 030 slice «Закулисье».
- Linking a column's author to the expert's page — `blocked_by` 031 (expert page); slice 1 shows the expert's name as plain text.
- Experts writing their own columns in a cabinet (REQ-124 in full) — a later 048 slice.
- Images, video or embeds inside the body of a material; the cover is the material's only image.
- A full «all news» archive page; a feed on the doctor storefront; comments, likes, view counters, subscriptions.
- The Academy home re-layout and the final placement of the rail — 029; slice 1 places the rail in today's home (EARS-14).
- Roles narrower than `platform_admin` — 039.

## Constraints

- Materials are platform domain data written through `apps/api` from `apps/admin`, never through Payload `apps/cms` (ADR-0004 design §10.1).
- The rail reads only the feed-entry store; nothing on the home holds a material of its own (PRD acceptance «the 029 home … holds no materials of its own»).
- A material's body is a structured rich-text document restricted to one allowlisted node and mark set; it is never stored or rendered as raw HTML.
- The doctor storefront (`apps/doctor`) serves no feed, no rail and no reading page and imports no feed or material contract (ADR-0016 §8).
- A slug is system-generated and permanent after the first publish (012 authoring contract).
- Only card data and the material itself are stored; no network data exists in slice 1.

## Event Model

**Commands** — `CreateMaterial`, `UpdateMaterial` (kind, title, author, partner, cover, body), `PublishMaterial`, `UnpublishMaterial` (platform administrator).

**Events** — `MaterialCreated`, `MaterialUpdated`, `MaterialPublished`, `MaterialUnpublished`, each a 010 change-audit row under the request's actor; `FeedEntryWritten`, `FeedEntryRemoved` — the feed-entry consequences of a publish, an edit of a published material and an unpublish, in the same transaction.

**Read models** — the admin material list and detail; the public feed page (cards, newest first, with a continuation cursor); the public material.

**Policies** — «a material is public only while it is published and, for a column, its expert is published, judged at the read»; «the feed is read from feed entries and the directory records they reference, never from materials»; «a partner is marked only while its organisation is published and shows its name»; «a published material has exactly one feed entry, an unpublished one none»; «the card date is the first publication instant and never moves on edit»; «the body is an allowlisted document, never HTML»; «the doctor storefront reads nothing of the feed».

## EARS requirements

> Flat numbering per ADR-0006 §4. Every clause realizes one or more PRD stories and is covered by `048-scenarios.feature`.

- **EARS-1** _(realizes: US-1)_ — WHEN a platform administrator creates or edits a material on the «Материалы» full-page form, THE SYSTEM SHALL persist the retained `materials` row with the kind — «Новость» (`news`) or «Колонка эксперта» (`column`) —, the title (trimmed 1–160), the optional partner of EARS-19, the optional normalized cover of the 012 media contract and the body of EARS-2; for a news item the optional author line «Автор» (trimmed 0–120, plain text), for a column the required expert chosen from the non-retired experts of the 012 directory, the author line then stored empty; a new material SHALL be a draft; a refused write SHALL show its field-addressed reasons on the form and change nothing.
- **EARS-2** _(realizes: US-1, US-8)_ — WHEN the rich-text editor of the material form edits the body, THE SYSTEM SHALL offer only the allowlisted set — paragraphs, headings of levels 2 and 3, bold, italic, bulleted and numbered lists, block quotes, line breaks and links — and SHALL strip anything else on paste; WHEN a body is written, THE SYSTEM SHALL store it as a structured document of exactly that set and SHALL refuse with a field error, storing nothing, a body containing another node or mark, a link whose target is not an absolute `https:`, `http:` or `mailto:` address or is longer than 2048 characters, more than 60 000 characters of text, more than 2 000 nodes or nesting deeper than 6 levels.
- **EARS-3** _(realizes: US-1)_ — WHEN a platform administrator chooses «Предпросмотр» on the material form, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the current, possibly unsaved, form content as the reading page of EARS-12 renders it, marked «Предпросмотр — материал не опубликован», without saving, publishing or changing the feed.
- **EARS-4** _(realizes: US-1, US-6)_ — WHEN a platform administrator publishes a draft material («Опубликовать»), THE SYSTEM SHALL refuse with `409 PUBLISH_REQUIREMENTS_NOT_MET` and field errors naming each missing item — the title, a body with at least one character of text and, for a column, a published expert — and otherwise SHALL, in one transaction, set `first_published_at` once, transition the material to `published` and write its feed entry; the material SHALL be the first card of the rail on the next read unless a material with a later first publication exists.
- **EARS-5** _(realizes: US-2)_ — WHEN a platform administrator saves an edit of a published material, THE SYSTEM SHALL apply it under the material's `If-Match` version and, in the same transaction, rewrite its feed entry, so the card and the reading page show the change on their next read; the card date SHALL stay the first publication instant; an edit that would leave the title or the body text of EARS-4 missing SHALL be refused with the same code and change nothing; the published state of a column's expert SHALL NOT be re-checked on edit, so an edit of a published column whose expert is not published SHALL be accepted, the column staying absent from the site by the read-time rule of EARS-9 and EARS-11 until its expert is published again.
- **EARS-6** _(realizes: US-2)_ — WHEN a platform administrator unpublishes a published material («Снять с публикации») and confirms, THE SYSTEM SHALL, in one transaction, return it to `draft` with its content kept and remove its feed entry, so it leaves the rail and its reading page answers not found on the next read; WHEN the administrator publishes it again, THE SYSTEM SHALL restore its card at its first publication instant.
- **EARS-7** _(realizes: US-1, US-2)_ — WHEN a platform administrator opens «Материалы», THE SYSTEM SHALL list every material on the `AdminDataList` composition with its title, kind, status, first publication date and author, most recently changed first, with search by title and filters by kind and status (012 EARS-23), the action «Новый материал» and an empty state when there is none; a published column whose expert is no longer published SHALL be marked «Не на сайте: эксперт не опубликован».
- **EARS-8** _(realizes: US-1, US-2)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL serve the «Материалы» section and every admin material endpoint only to a `platform_admin` in the dedicated admin session with the second factor (011, 047), with CSRF, and SHALL refuse anyone else server-side and hide the section from their navigation; every mutating endpoint SHALL require a canonical UUID idempotency key and, except for create, the current `If-Match` version per 012 EARS-17, refusing a stale version with `412 PRECONDITION_FAILED`; every change to a material SHALL be recorded in the 010 change audit under the acting administrator.
- **EARS-9** _(realizes: US-6, US-10)_ — WHEN any caller reads `GET /v1/public/feed` with an optional `cursor` and a `limit` of 1–24 (default 12), THE SYSTEM SHALL return the public feed entries newest first by publication instant, ties by id, as cards of the common shape — `id`, `kind` (`news` | `column` in slice 1), `title`, `publishedAt`, `image` (`{ url }` or `null`), `source` (`{ platform: "academy", name }`, the name «Академия» for a news item and, for a column, the expert's current name as «Фамилия И. О.» — the family name and the initials of the given name and the patronymic —, built at the moment of the read from the 012 name parts), `partner` (`{ name }` or `null`, EARS-19) and `href` (`/materials/{slug}`) — with no body, and `nextCursor`, `null` after the last entry; a column whose expert is not published at the moment of the read SHALL be absent; a malformed cursor SHALL be refused with `400`.
- **EARS-10** _(realizes: US-10)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL serve the feed read of EARS-9 from the feed-entry store, in which every entry names its source platform and its source record, joined only to the directory records an entry references — the 012 expert of its author and the 032 organisation of its partner — for their public state and current names at the moment of the read, and never to the materials table; the own-materials writer of EARS-4…EARS-6 being the only writer in slice 1; no field of a card SHALL be specific to the own-materials source, and the feed card SHALL render from the card alone.
- **EARS-11** _(realizes: US-8)_ — WHEN any caller reads `GET /v1/public/materials/{slug}`, THE SYSTEM SHALL answer a public material with its slug, kind, title, `publishedAt`, cover URL or `null`, the author — the news author line or the column expert's current public display name, with no expert slug or link —, the partner of EARS-19 and its body document; a draft, unknown or non-public material SHALL be answered `404` alike.
- **EARS-12** _(realizes: US-8)_ — WHEN a visitor, signed in or not, opens `academy.doctor.school/materials/{slug}` of a public material, THE SYSTEM SHALL render the kind («Новость» / «Колонка эксперта»), the title, the publication date, the author as plain text, under the byline the mark «Партнёрский материал · <name>» when the material read carries `partner` (EARS-19) and no mark without it, the cover when there is one, and the body through the shared rich-text renderer, with no raw HTML, every link to another host opening in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, a way back to the Academy home, and the material's title as the document title; WHEN the material is not public, THE SYSTEM SHALL render the Academy not-found page; WHEN the read fails, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the error state with «Повторить».
- **EARS-13** _(realizes: US-6)_ — WHEN the shared feed card renders a card, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the image or, without one, the no-image layout; the kind mark «Новость» or «Колонка эксперта»; the title clamped to the lines the card holds; the source — «Академия» with the Academy logo for a news item, the expert's name «Фамилия И. О.» of EARS-9 with the initials avatar for a column —; and the date as day and month (with the year when it is not the current one, in Moscow time); the whole card SHALL be one keyboard-reachable link to its `href` in the same tab; slice 1 SHALL show no duration and no network mark, and the partner mark as EARS-19 states.
- **EARS-14** _(realizes: US-6)_ — WHEN a visitor opens the Academy home, THE SYSTEM SHALL render, after the events block, the section «Новости, блог и видео» with the kicker «Соцсети и блог» and the line «Что выходит в Академии и в наших соцсетях», and the rail of the first EARS-9 page: on a desktop two rows of cards with the next column partly visible and the buttons ← («Назад к более новым материалам») and → («Дальше, к более ранним материалам») always shown, ← disabled at the start and → disabled at the end once nothing more is to load, each press moving by the visible width; on a phone one row with the next card partly visible, moved by swipe, with no buttons; the rail SHALL be focusable, move one card on ArrowLeft / ArrowRight and to its ends on Home / End, move by mouse drag on a desktop, scroll without animation under reduced motion, and SHALL never move, rotate or play anything by itself.
- **EARS-15** _(realizes: US-6, US-9)_ — WHEN the rail is scrolled to within one and a half cards of its end and the last page carried a `nextCursor`, THE SYSTEM SHALL request the next page (8 cards on a desktop, 6 on a phone), show trailing placeholder cards while it loads, mark the rail busy and announce «Загружаем более ранние материалы», then «Загружено материалов: N» and, after the last page, «Это все материалы.»; WHEN the next page fails, THE SYSTEM SHALL keep every loaded card, announce «Не удалось загрузить более ранние материалы» and offer «Повторить» at the end of the rail.
- **EARS-16** _(realizes: US-9)_ — WHILE the first feed page is loading, THE SYSTEM SHALL show placeholder cards (10 on a desktop, 3 on a phone) with both buttons disabled; WHEN the feed has no entry, THE SYSTEM SHALL show «Записей пока нет.» with «Здесь появятся новости Академии и публикации из наших соцсетей.» and no rail and no buttons; WHEN the first page cannot be read, THE SYSTEM SHALL show «Не удалось загрузить новости.» as an alert with «Повторить», which reads the first page again; the rest of the home SHALL keep working in each case, and no state SHALL show an invented entry.
- **EARS-17** _(realizes: US-6, US-8)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL serve the rail and the reading page only on the Academy host: the doctor storefront SHALL have no `/materials` route, render no feed, and import no feed or material contract, and doctor-storefront requests for `/materials/{slug}` SHALL get its not-found page.
- **EARS-18** _(realizes: US-1, US-6, US-8)_ — WHILE the viewport is a phone or a narrow screen, THE SYSTEM SHALL keep the rail, the reading page, the admin material list and the material form with its editor usable without horizontal scrolling of the page, with every card, button and editor control keyboard-reachable, and each of these screens SHALL pass the platform's `playwright-axe` gate.
- **EARS-19** _(realizes: US-1, US-6)_ — WHEN a platform administrator sets or clears the optional «Партнёр» of a material on its form — an organisation chosen from the non-retired organisations of the 032 registry —, THE SYSTEM SHALL store the reference on the material under EARS-1, EARS-5 and EARS-8 and, for a published material, on its feed entry in the same transaction, refusing a retired or unknown organisation with a field error and changing nothing; WHILE the referenced organisation is published and its name rule «Показывать название публично» (032 EARS-5) is on, the feed card of EARS-9 and the material of EARS-11 SHALL carry `partner: { name }` with the organisation's current public title, judged at the moment of the read, and otherwise `partner: null`, the material staying public either way; WHEN the shared feed card renders a card whose `partner` is set, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the mark «Партнёрский материал · <name>», and a card without a partner SHALL show no mark.

## Invariants

- Nothing on the Academy home or the reading page shows a material that is not public; a public material is published and, for a column, has a published expert (EARS-4, EARS-6, EARS-9, EARS-11).
- A published material has exactly one feed entry and a draft has none, written in the transaction of the change (EARS-4, EARS-5, EARS-6).
- The card date is the first publication instant; no edit and no re-publish moves a card (EARS-5, EARS-6).
- The feed is read from feed entries and the expert and organisation records they reference, never from materials, and a card carries nothing that only an own material has (EARS-10).
- No partner mark names an organisation that is not published or whose name rule is off (EARS-19).
- No body is stored or rendered as HTML; every stored body belongs to the allowlisted set (EARS-2, EARS-12).
- No card or page links to a page that does not exist: an own card leads to its reading page, and the author links nowhere before 031 (EARS-11, EARS-12, EARS-13).
- Nothing in the rail moves or plays by itself (EARS-14).
- Every material write is made by a `platform_admin` with the second factor and leaves one 010 audit row (EARS-8).

## Slice 2 — Telegram channel connector

### Outcomes (slice 2)

- A platform administrator keeps the list of official accounts the feed reads: platform, address, on / off; a Telegram account that cannot be read shows as failing in that list.
- A new or edited post of a listed, switched-on Telegram channel reaches the rail by itself on the next read after Telegram delivers it; nobody copies posts by hand.
- The channel's posts published before the bot joined are brought in once by a one-off import, without duplicating a post the webhook delivered.
- A Telegram card shows the post's first lines, its preview and, for a video, its duration, with the source «BBM Academy · Telegram»; it opens the original on `t.me` in a new tab and nothing plays inside the Academy.
- A platform administrator hides one post from the rail, or switches a source off, without deleting anything; a Telegram outage never errors the rail.
- The rail, the card contract and the slice-1 materials stay as built: the connector adds one card member and rows of its platform (US-10).

### Scope (slice 2)

- The «Источники» page of the «Материалы» area of `apps/admin` (source list, add, edit address, switch off / on, status) and the source's «Публикации» page (the source's posts with «Скрыть из ленты» / «Вернуть в ленту»), approved-non-canvas.
- The `feed_sources` and `feed_entry_messages` tables and the slice-2 columns of `feed_entries` in `packages/db` with their forward migration; the admin source and hide endpoints, the Telegram webhook, the Telegram Bot API client, the hourly source check and the one-off import command in `apps/api`; the `post` card member and the source contract in `packages/schemas`.
- The `post` variant of the shared `FeedCard` in `@ds/design-system`, rendered inside the slice-1 rail (ADR-0013 §A1, existing block before a new one).
- The ADR-0011 §2.2 channel #10 (Telegram Bot API) and the webhook ingress on the public API host.

#### Out of scope (slice 2)

- VK posts and VK Видео — slice 3 of 048, #3944; RuTube — slice 4; Dzen — slice 5; YouTube and Instagram — out of the starting set (PRD).
- Removing a source row: an account is switched off, never deleted (US-3).
- A partner on a network post: a Telegram card carries `partner: null`.
- Reading channel history through the Bot API (it has none) or through MTProto (a user-account client: a different product and auth); posts published while a source was off or while the webhook was unreachable for more than 24 hours come back only by a re-run of the import.
- Playing a video or embedding the Telegram post widget inside the Academy (US-7).
- The production run of the import, the bot setup and the webhook registration — a deferred production action, its own work-package row below, not an EARS clause.

### Constraints (slice 2)

- Telegram is reached only through ADR-0011 §2.2 channel #10: `https://api.telegram.org` for the Bot API methods `setWebhook`, `getWebhookInfo`, `getChat`, `getChatMember`, `getMe`, `getFile` and the file download of a preview image; nothing else is sent to Telegram, and no personal data is.
- Only card data is stored (Bot Developer ToS §4.3): no raw update payload is kept beyond its processing, no video file is fetched, and `author_signature` is never stored.
- The bot token and the webhook secret are server-side configuration only: they appear in no log line, error, response, admin screen or stored row.
- The feed read still reads only `feed_entries` and the directory records of EARS-10; a source's on / off state and a hidden post are written onto its entries in the transaction of the change, so the read stays a single-store read and never calls Telegram.
- The Bot API sends no update when a channel post is deleted ([Update](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api#update) lists `channel_post` and `edited_channel_post` and no channel deletion update; the only deletion update is `deleted_business_messages`). **Known limit:** a post deleted in the channel stays in the rail until a platform administrator hides it (EARS-32). **Known limit (history):** the import of EARS-30 keeps only messages that carry text, so a photo or video posted with no caption before the webhook was registered is not brought in; the import counts such messages on their own line.
- Telegram keeps an undelivered update for at most 24 hours ([Getting updates](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api#getting-updates)).

### Event Model (slice 2)

**Commands** — `AddFeedSource`, `EditFeedSourceAddress`, `SwitchFeedSourceOff`, `SwitchFeedSourceOn`, `HideFeedEntry`, `UnhideFeedEntry` (platform administrator); `RegisterTelegramWebhook`, `ImportTelegramHistory` (operator command); `CheckFeedSources` (hourly schedule).

**Events** — `TelegramPostReceived`, `TelegramPostEdited` (webhook); `FeedEntryWritten`, `FeedEntryUpdated` (connector or import); `FeedSourceSwitchedOff` / `FeedSourceSwitchedOn`, `FeedEntryHidden` / `FeedEntryUnhidden`, each a 010 change-audit row under the acting administrator; `FeedSourceChecked` (status `ok` or `failing` with a reason); `TelegramUpdateDropped` (a log line with its reason, never a stored row).

**Read models** — the admin source list with status; the source's posts list; the public feed page, now with `post` cards.

**Policies** — «only a post of a listed, switched-on channel becomes an entry»; «a post is one entry, an album is one entry»; «an edit rewrites the entry in place and never moves its date»; «an entry of a switched-off source or a hidden entry is on no public surface and is kept»; «an import never writes a post that already has an entry»; «the feed read never calls a platform»; «no secret, raw payload or post author name is stored or logged».

### EARS requirements (slice 2)

> Flat numbering continues after EARS-19 (ADR-0006 §4). Every clause realizes one or more PRD stories and is covered by `048-scenarios.feature`.

- **EARS-20** _(realizes: US-3)_ — WHEN a platform administrator opens «Материалы» → «Источники», THE SYSTEM SHALL list every feed source on the `AdminDataList` composition with its platform («Telegram» in slice 2), its address as `t.me/<имя>`, its state «Включён» / «Выключен», its status of EARS-31 with the time of the last check, the number of its posts in the feed, the actions «Добавить источник», «Изменить адрес», «Выключить» / «Включить» and «Публикации», and the empty state «Источников пока нет.» when there is none.
- **EARS-21** _(realizes: US-3)_ — WHEN a platform administrator adds a source or edits its address, THE SYSTEM SHALL accept a platform among the connected networks (only «Telegram» in slice 2) and an address given as `https://t.me/<имя>`, `t.me/<имя>`, `@<имя>` or `<имя>`, store it as the lowercase public username (5–32 Latin letters, digits and underscores, starting with a letter) and store a new source switched on with the status «Не проверен»; THE SYSTEM SHALL refuse with a field error, storing nothing, an address of another form, a private-channel invite link and an address already listed for that platform; WHEN the address of a source that has entries changes, THE SYSTEM SHALL rewrite the `href` of each of its entries in the same transaction; WHEN a source is added, edited or switched on, THE SYSTEM SHALL run the check of EARS-31 for it at once, so a newly added source has its Telegram id bound before any import (EARS-30).
- **EARS-22** _(realizes: US-3)_ — WHEN a platform administrator switches a source off («Выключить») and confirms, THE SYSTEM SHALL, in one transaction, mark the source off and mark each of its entries as belonging to a switched-off source, so its posts leave the rail on the next read and stay listed in the admin, and from then on SHALL drop every update of that channel (EARS-25); WHEN the administrator switches it on («Включить»), THE SYSTEM SHALL, in one transaction, clear that mark, so its kept entries that are not hidden return to the rail at their own dates, and accept its new posts again; posts published while the source was off SHALL NOT be recovered except by a re-run of the import of EARS-30.
- **EARS-23** _(realizes: US-3, US-5)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL serve the «Источники» and «Публикации» pages and every admin source and hide endpoint only to a `platform_admin` in the dedicated admin session with the second factor, with CSRF, refusing anyone else server-side and hiding the pages from their navigation, and SHALL apply the idempotency key, the `If-Match` precondition and the 010 change audit of EARS-8 to every mutating source or hide endpoint.
- **EARS-24** _(realizes: US-4)_ — WHEN Telegram calls `POST /v1/integrations/telegram/webhook`, THE SYSTEM SHALL compare the `X-Telegram-Bot-Api-Secret-Token` header with the configured webhook secret in constant time before reading the body, and SHALL answer `401`, processing and storing nothing, to a request whose header is absent or differs; WHILE the bot token or the webhook secret is not configured, THE SYSTEM SHALL answer `404` on the webhook route; WHEN an operator runs the webhook registration command, THE SYSTEM SHALL call `setWebhook` with the environment's public webhook URL, the configured `secret_token` and `allowed_updates` = `["channel_post", "edited_channel_post"]`.
- **EARS-25** _(realizes: US-4)_ — WHEN an authenticated webhook request carries a `channel_post` or an `edited_channel_post`, THE SYSTEM SHALL match its channel to a Telegram source by the channel's Telegram id once bound, else by its username, binding the id on the first match, and SHALL process the post only when the matched source is switched on; an update of an unlisted or switched-off channel, of another update type or with a malformed body SHALL be answered `200`, dropped and logged with its update id, channel id, message id and drop reason only; a repeated delivery of a post already processed SHALL change nothing; WHEN processing fails on our side (the database is unavailable), THE SYSTEM SHALL answer `500`, so that Telegram delivers the update again, and write nothing partial.
- **EARS-26** _(realizes: US-4, US-6, US-10)_ — WHEN a processed channel post is a text post, a photo, a video or a part of an album of photos and videos, THE SYSTEM SHALL write one feed entry of kind `post` of the Telegram platform with: the text — the post's `text` or `caption` as plain text, formatting dropped, whitespace collapsed, cut at a word boundary to 500 characters with «…»; the publication instant — the message `date`; the preview of EARS-29; for a video, the duration `video.duration` in seconds; the permalink `https://t.me/<address>/<message_id>`; no partner; and SHALL drop and log, writing nothing, a forwarded post (`forward_origin` set), a post with any other content (poll, sticker, animation, audio, voice, document, location, contact, story, paid media, giveaway, game), a service message and a post that has neither text nor an image.
- **EARS-27** _(realizes: US-4, US-6)_ — WHEN the parts of one album (one `media_group_id`) arrive as separate updates, THE SYSTEM SHALL keep exactly one entry for the album, attaching each part's message id to it, with its text from the part that carries a caption, its preview, duration and permalink from the part with the smallest message id, and its publication instant the earliest `date` of its parts.
- **EARS-28** _(realizes: US-4)_ — WHEN an `edited_channel_post` arrives for a post that has an entry, THE SYSTEM SHALL rewrite that entry's text and, when the edited part's media changed, its preview, in place, keeping its publication instant, its hidden state and its place in the rail; WHEN it arrives for a post that has no entry, THE SYSTEM SHALL process it as a new post under EARS-26.
- **EARS-29** _(realizes: US-4, US-6)_ — WHEN an entry needs a preview — the largest photo size at most 1280 px wide (the smallest size when none is) of a photo, or the `video.thumbnail` of a video —, THE SYSTEM SHALL fetch it through `getFile` and its download link, refusing a file over 20 MB, normalize it with the 012 still-image normalizer and store it in our object storage under a server-generated key, never storing a Telegram file link; WHEN the fetch or the normalization fails, THE SYSTEM SHALL keep the entry in the feed without an image, retry the preview on a schedule for at least 24 hours, set it on the first success and, after the last attempt, leave the card in its no-image layout; a failed preview SHALL NOT mark the source failing.
- **EARS-30** _(realizes: US-4)_ — WHEN an operator runs the one-off import command with a Telegram source and the machine-readable JSON of the Telegram Desktop «Export chat history» of that channel, THE SYSTEM SHALL validate the whole file before writing — refusing, with nothing written and a non-zero exit, a file that does not parse, is not a channel export or names another channel than the source's bound Telegram id, and refusing the same way, with the printed reason «the source has no bound Telegram id; run the source check (EARS-31)», while the source has none, so the channel comparison is never skipped — and then SHALL write one `post` entry per exported message under the mapping of EARS-26, its preview from the exported photo or video thumbnail file when present (EARS-29), skipping a message with no text (a photo or video with no caption included, Constraints), a forward, a service message and other content; a message whose id already has an entry of that source SHALL be skipped, so a re-run and a post the webhook delivered are never duplicated or overwritten; the command SHALL print the counts read, written, skipped as existing, skipped as media with no text, dropped by reason and previews failed.
- **EARS-31** _(realizes: US-3)_ — WHEN the hourly source check or the check of EARS-21 runs for a switched-on Telegram source, THE SYSTEM SHALL read the channel through `getChat`, the bot's membership through `getChatMember` and the webhook state through `getWebhookInfo`, and SHALL set the source's status to «Работает» or to «Ошибка» with one reason — «Telegram не подключён на сервере», «Канал не найден или бот не добавлен в канал», «Бот не состоит в канале», «Адрес канала изменился на t.me/<имя>», «Вебхук Telegram не зарегистрирован» or «Telegram не может доставить новые посты» —, with the time of the check; WHILE the source has no bound Telegram id, THE SYSTEM SHALL read the channel as `getChat(@<address>)` and, when the returned `username` equals the source address ignoring case, bind the returned `id` to the source in the same write as its status; WHEN Telegram is unreachable during a check, THE SYSTEM SHALL set «Ошибка» with «Telegram недоступен» and change no entry.
- **EARS-32** _(realizes: US-5)_ — WHEN a platform administrator opens «Публикации» of a source, THE SYSTEM SHALL list the source's entries newest first with the first line of the text, the date, the state «В ленте» / «Скрыт» / «Источник выключен» and a link to the original opening in a new tab; WHEN the administrator chooses «Скрыть из ленты» on an entry and confirms, THE SYSTEM SHALL mark it hidden, so it leaves the rail on the next read and stays listed, and an edit of the post SHALL NOT bring it back; WHEN the administrator chooses «Вернуть в ленту», THE SYSTEM SHALL clear the mark and the entry returns at its own date; a post deleted in the channel SHALL leave the rail only this way (Constraints, known limit).
- **EARS-33** _(realizes: US-6, US-7, US-10)_ — WHEN any caller reads `GET /v1/public/feed`, THE SYSTEM SHALL serve a Telegram entry as a card of kind `post` — `id`, `kind: "post"`, `text`, `publishedAt`, `image` (`{ url }` signed at the read, or `null`), `duration` (seconds or `null`), `source` (`{ platform: "telegram", name: "BBM Academy · Telegram" }`), `partner: null`, `href` (the absolute `https://t.me/…` permalink) and `external: true` — in the order, cursor and page of EARS-9, leaving the `news` and `column` members unchanged; a hidden entry and an entry of a switched-off source SHALL be absent.
- **EARS-34** _(realizes: US-6, US-7)_ — WHEN the shared feed card renders a `post` card, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the image — with a play mark and the duration as `m:ss` (`h:mm:ss` from one hour) for a video — or, without one, the no-image layout with the field mark «Telegram»; the source «BBM Academy · Telegram» with the Academy logo and the network chip «TG» named «Telegram»; no kind badge; the post text clamped to the lines the card holds; the date of EARS-13; and «Telegram ↗» with the accessible text «, откроется в новой вкладке на площадке Telegram»; the whole card SHALL be one keyboard-reachable link to its `href` opening in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, and nothing SHALL play inside the Academy.
- **EARS-35** _(realizes: US-9)_ — WHILE Telegram is unreachable, the webhook is not delivered, a source is failing or the connector is not configured, THE SYSTEM SHALL serve the feed read and the rail from the stored entries with no error state for that source, the rail showing the stored Telegram cards and every other card as usual.
- **EARS-36** _(realizes: US-4)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL keep the bot token and the webhook secret in server-side configuration only, redacting the token from every outbound URL it logs, and SHALL write no log line, error, response, stored row or admin screen that carries either; of a Telegram post it SHALL store only the card fields of EARS-26, the message ids and `media_group_id` of EARS-27 and the preview of EARS-29 with its operational state (`preview_file_id`, `preview_file_unique_id`, `preview_state`, `preview_attempts`) — never the raw update, the `author_signature`, a video file or a Telegram file link —, and SHALL log each Bot API call with its method, HTTP status, duration and outcome only.
- **EARS-37** _(realizes: US-3, US-5, US-6)_ — WHILE the viewport is a phone or a narrow screen, THE SYSTEM SHALL keep the «Источники» and «Публикации» pages usable without horizontal scrolling of the page with every action keyboard-reachable, and both pages and the Academy home carrying a Telegram card SHALL pass the platform's `playwright-axe` gate.

### Invariants (slice 2)

- Only a post of a listed, switched-on Telegram channel, delivered with the right secret or imported by the operator, becomes an entry (EARS-24, EARS-25, EARS-30).
- A Telegram post or album has at most one entry; no delivery, edit or import re-run duplicates it (EARS-25, EARS-27, EARS-28, EARS-30).
- An entry's date is the post's Telegram date; no edit moves it (EARS-26, EARS-28).
- No hidden entry and no entry of a switched-off source is on a public surface, and neither is deleted (EARS-22, EARS-32, EARS-33).
- The feed read never calls Telegram, and no Telegram failure errors the rail (EARS-33, EARS-35).
- A Telegram card always opens its original on `t.me` in a new tab; nothing plays inside the Academy (EARS-34).
- No bot token, webhook secret, raw update, post author name, video file or Telegram file link is stored or logged (EARS-36).
- Only the Bot API methods of ADR-0011 §2.2 channel #10 are called, with no personal data (EARS-24, EARS-29, EARS-31, EARS-36).

## Slice 3 — VK connector (wall posts and VK Видео)

### Outcomes (slice 3)

- A platform administrator adds the BBM Academy VK community to the slice-2 source list (platform «VK», address `vk.com/<имя>`); a community that cannot be read, or whose Callback events stopped arriving, shows as failing in that list.
- A new wall post or a new VK Видео video of the listed, switched-on community reaches the rail by itself on the next read after VK delivers it; nobody copies posts by hand.
- An edit of a wall post in VK shows on its card, and a post deleted from the wall leaves the rail, within the hourly reconcile, with no administrator action.
- The wall's earlier posts are brought in once by a one-off import, without duplicating a post the Callback delivered.
- A VK post card shows the post's first lines, its preview and, for a video, its duration, with the source «BBM Academy · VK»; a VK Видео card shows the video's title, cover and duration with the source «BBM Academy · VK Видео»; both open the original on `vk.com` in a new tab, and nothing plays inside the Academy.
- One VK publication is one card: a video that is also attached to a wall post shows once, as the post.
- The rail, the source list, the hide action and the slice-1 and slice-2 cards stay as built: the connector adds one platform, one card member and rows of its platform (US-10).

### Scope (slice 3)

- The platform «VK» in the slice-2 «Источники» page (`Select` option, address forms, VK status reasons, the month's VK call count) and the state «Удалён в VK» in «Публикации», approved-non-canvas.
- The slice-3 enum values, the `vk_group_id` column of `feed_sources`, the `removed_at` column of `feed_entries` and the `feed_entry_vk_objects` table in `packages/db` with their forward migration; the VK Callback route, the VK API client, the post and video mapping, the hourly reconcile, the VK source check and the one-off import command in `apps/api`; the `post` member's `platform: "vk"` and the new `video` member of the card contract in `packages/schemas`.
- The VK forms of the `post` variant and the new `video` variant of the shared `FeedCard` in `@ds/design-system`, rendered inside the slice-1 rail (ADR-0013 §A1).
- The ADR-0011 §2.2 channel #11 (VK API) and the Callback ingress on the public API host.

#### Out of scope (slice 3)

- VK clips: VK documents no read route for clips, so slice 3 reads none; a clip posted on the wall as a wall post is a post (PRD «Open questions»).
- Standalone videos uploaded before the Callback server was configured: `video.get` is gated behind a scope VK grants on request, so they are not imported (Constraints).
- Reposts, scheduled and suggested posts, comments, live streams, VK stories, music videos and films.
- The VK business-profile verification: the slice's call volume stays far under the unverified cap (Constraints).
- RuTube — slice 4; Dzen — slice 5.
- Playing a video or embedding the VK post widget or player inside the Academy (US-7).
- The VK app and service key, the Callback server setup in the community settings and the production run of the import — a deferred production action, its own work-package row below, not an EARS clause.

### Constraints (slice 3)

- VK is reached only through ADR-0011 §2.2 channel #11: `https://api.vk.com/method/*` for the methods `wall.get` and `groups.getById` with a pinned API version; nothing else is sent to VK, and no personal data is.
- Only card data is stored: no raw Callback event is kept beyond its processing, no video or image file is fetched or copied, and the post fields `created_by`, `signer_id` and `from_id` are never stored.
- The VK service key, the Callback secret and the confirmation string are server-side configuration only: they appear in no log line, error, response, admin screen or stored row.
- The feed read still reads only `feed_entries` and the directory records of EARS-10; a removed post is written onto its entry by the reconcile, so the read stays a single-store read and never calls VK.
- VK sends no event when a wall post is edited or deleted, nor when a video is edited or deleted ([community events](https://dev.vk.com/ru/api/community-events/json-schema) list `wall_post_new`, `wall_repost`, `wall_schedule_*`, `wall_reply_*`, `video_new` and `video_comment_*`, and no edit or delete event). Wall edits and deletions are therefore seen by the hourly reconcile of EARS-45. **Known limit:** a video entry of `video_new` is never re-read (`video.get` needs a user key with the `video` scope, granted only on request — [video.get](https://dev.vk.com/ru/method/video.get)), so an edited or deleted standalone video stays as delivered until a platform administrator hides it (EARS-32). **Known limit (history):** standalone videos published before the Callback server was configured are not imported (EARS-46).
- VK retries an event that is not answered `ok` with HTTP 200 after 10 seconds, 3 minutes, 10 minutes, 30 minutes and 1 hour, and stops sending for a while after repeated errors ([Callback API](https://dev.vk.com/ru/api/callback/getting-started)); a wall post lost past that window comes back with the next reconcile, a video only by an administrator's re-upload.
- Per VK ID business-cabinet account, an unverified business profile may make 10 000 VK API method calls a month ([Rules](https://dev.vk.com/ru/rules)); slice 3 makes about 1 500 per source a month (one reconcile and one source check an hour) plus a one-off import of a few pages. Whether incoming Callback deliveries count against the cap is **UNCONFIRMED**; the wording counts «вызовов методов», our outbound calls.

### Event Model (slice 3)

**Commands** — `AddFeedSource`, `EditFeedSourceAddress`, `SwitchFeedSourceOff`, `SwitchFeedSourceOn`, `HideFeedEntry`, `UnhideFeedEntry` of slice 2, now for VK too (platform administrator); `ImportVkHistory` (operator command); `ReconcileVkWall`, `CheckFeedSources` (hourly schedule).

**Events** — `VkConfirmationAnswered`, `VkWallPostReceived`, `VkVideoReceived` (Callback); `FeedEntryWritten`, `FeedEntryUpdated`, `FeedEntryAbsorbed` (a video entry folded into its post), `FeedEntryRemovedAtSource`, `FeedEntryRestoredAtSource` (connector, reconcile or import); the slice-2 switch and hide events; `FeedSourceChecked`; `VkEventDropped` (a log line with its reason, never a stored row).

**Read models** — the admin source list with VK status and the month's VK call count; the source's posts list with «Удалён в VK»; the public feed page, now with VK `post` cards and `video` cards.

**Policies** — «only an event with the right secret of a listed, bound, switched-on community becomes an entry»; «one VK publication is one entry; a video attached to a post is the post»; «an edit seen by the reconcile rewrites the entry in place and never moves its date»; «a post gone from the wall leaves the rail and is kept; a post that reappears returns»; «an import never writes a publication that already has an entry»; «the feed read never calls a platform»; «no secret, raw event, image file or post author is stored or logged».

### EARS requirements (slice 3)

> Flat numbering continues after EARS-37 (ADR-0006 §4). Every clause realizes one or more PRD stories and is covered by `048-scenarios.feature`.

- **EARS-38** _(realizes: US-3)_ — WHEN a platform administrator adds a source of the platform «VK» or edits a VK source's address on «Источники», THE SYSTEM SHALL accept an address given as `https://vk.com/<имя>`, `vk.com/<имя>`, `https://vk.ru/<имя>`, `vk.ru/<имя>` or `<имя>`, where `<имя>` is the community's screen name (2–32 Latin letters, digits, dots and underscores) or `club<id>` / `public<id>`, store it as the lowercase screen name, list it as `vk.com/<имя>` with the platform «VK», and store a new source switched on with the status «Не проверен»; THE SYSTEM SHALL refuse with a field error, storing nothing, an address of another form (a wall, a post, a video or a user page link) and an address already listed for VK; WHEN a VK source is added, edited or switched on, THE SYSTEM SHALL run the check of EARS-44 for it at once, so its VK community id is bound before any Callback event is processed or any import runs; the `href` of a VK entry SHALL be built from the bound community id, so an address edit rewrites none.
- **EARS-39** _(realizes: US-4)_ — WHEN VK calls `POST /v1/integrations/vk/callback`, THE SYSTEM SHALL read the body bounded at 1 MB, compare its `secret` with the configured Callback secret in constant time and answer `401`, processing and storing nothing, to a body that does not parse, carries a different `secret` or, for any event but `confirmation`, carries none; WHILE the service key, the Callback secret or the confirmation string is not configured, THE SYSTEM SHALL answer `404` on the route; WHEN a `confirmation` event with no `secret` or the right one names the `group_id` bound to a listed VK source, THE SYSTEM SHALL answer the configured confirmation string as plain text with HTTP `200`, and a `confirmation` of any other group SHALL be answered `ok`, dropped and logged.
- **EARS-40** _(realizes: US-4)_ — WHEN an authenticated Callback event is not a `confirmation`, THE SYSTEM SHALL match its `group_id` to the VK source bound to that community id and SHALL process the event only when it is `wall_post_new` or `video_new`, its object validates and the matched source is switched on; an event of an unlisted, unbound or switched-off community, of another type or with a malformed object SHALL be answered `ok` with HTTP `200`, dropped and logged with its event id, type, community id, object id, `X-Retry-Counter` and drop reason only; a repeated delivery of a publication already processed SHALL change nothing; WHEN processing fails on our side (the database is unavailable), THE SYSTEM SHALL answer `500`, so that VK delivers the event again, and write nothing partial.
- **EARS-41** _(realizes: US-4, US-6, US-10)_ — WHEN a processed wall post (`wall_post_new`, or a post read by EARS-45 or EARS-46) has `post_type` = `post` and an empty `copy_history`, THE SYSTEM SHALL write one feed entry of kind `post` of the VK platform with: the text — the post's `text` as plain text, whitespace collapsed, cut at a word boundary to 500 characters with «…»; the publication instant — the post `date`; the preview — the largest size at most 1280 px wide (the smallest size when none is) of the first photo attachment, else the largest cover image at most 1280 px wide of the first video attachment, referenced by its VK URL; the duration — that video's `duration` in seconds when the preview comes from a video, else none; the permalink `https://vk.com/wall-<group_id>_<post_id>`; no partner; and SHALL drop and log, writing nothing, a repost (`copy_history` not empty or `post_type` = `copy`), a post of `post_type` `postpone`, `suggest` or `reply`, and a post that has neither text nor a photo or video attachment.
- **EARS-42** _(realizes: US-4, US-6, US-10)_ — WHEN a processed `video_new` carries a video of the community (`owner_id` = −`group_id`) whose `type` is `video` and which is not a live stream, THE SYSTEM SHALL write one feed entry of kind `video` of the VK platform with: the title — the video's `title` trimmed and cut at a word boundary to 160 characters with «…»; the publication instant — the video `date`; the preview — the largest `image[]` size at most 1280 px wide (the smallest size when none is), referenced by its VK URL; the duration `duration` in seconds; the permalink `https://vk.com/video-<group_id>_<video_id>`; no partner; and SHALL drop and log, writing nothing, a video of another owner, a live stream (`live` set), a video of any other `type` and a video with no title.
- **EARS-43** _(realizes: US-4, US-6)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL keep at most one entry per VK publication, keyed by `(source, object kind, VK object id)`: WHEN a `video_new` arrives for a video already held as an attachment of a held post entry, THE SYSTEM SHALL write nothing; WHEN a post entry is written whose video attachments of the same community include a video held as a `video` entry, THE SYSTEM SHALL, in the same transaction, attach that video's key to the post entry and remove the video entry, the post entry taking the video entry's hidden mark, so the publication shows once, as the post.
- **EARS-44** _(realizes: US-3)_ — WHEN the hourly source check or the check of EARS-38 runs for a switched-on VK source, THE SYSTEM SHALL read the community through `groups.getById` and SHALL set the source's status to «Работает» or to «Ошибка» with one reason — «VK не подключён на сервере», «Сообщество не найдено», «Адрес сообщества изменился на vk.com/<имя>», «VK не присылает новые записи», «VK недоступен» or «Лимит вызовов VK почти исчерпан» —, with the time of the check; WHILE the source has no bound VK community id, THE SYSTEM SHALL bind the returned `id` to the source in the same write as its status when the returned `screen_name` equals the source address ignoring case or the address is `club<id>` / `public<id>` of that id; «VK не присылает новые записи» SHALL be set when the last reconcile wrote a post newer than the last Callback event received from that community and older than one hour; «Лимит вызовов VK почти исчерпан» SHALL be set when the calls of the current calendar month (Moscow time) reach 8 000; WHEN VK is unreachable during a check, THE SYSTEM SHALL set «Ошибка» with «VK недоступен» and change no entry; «Источники» SHALL show the VK calls of the current month beside the VK sources.
- **EARS-45** _(realizes: US-4, US-5)_ — WHEN the hourly reconcile runs for a switched-on VK source with a bound community id, THE SYSTEM SHALL read the wall through `wall.get` (`owner_id` = −`group_id`, `filter` = `owner`, `count` = 100) and SHALL, for the returned posts that EARS-41 maps: rewrite the text and preview of a held post whose text or preview changed, keeping its publication instant, its hidden mark and its switched-off mark; write a post that has no entry under EARS-41 and EARS-43; set the removed mark on a held post entry of the source whose date lies within the span of the returned non-pinned posts — the whole wall when fewer than 100 posts are returned — and which is absent from them; and clear the removed mark of an entry whose post is returned again; WHEN VK is unreachable or answers an error, THE SYSTEM SHALL change no entry and leave the status to EARS-44; a `video` entry SHALL NOT be reconciled (Constraints, known limit).
- **EARS-46** _(realizes: US-4)_ — WHEN an operator runs the one-off VK import command with a VK source, THE SYSTEM SHALL refuse, with nothing written, a non-zero exit and the printed reason «the source has no bound VK community id; run the source check (EARS-44)», a source with no bound community id, and otherwise SHALL read the whole wall through `wall.get` pages of 100 (`offset`) and write one `post` entry per post under EARS-41 and EARS-43; a post that already has an entry of that source SHALL be skipped, so a re-run and a post the Callback delivered are never duplicated or overwritten; the command SHALL stop before the call count of the month would pass 8 000 with the reason printed and print the counts read, written, skipped as existing, dropped by reason and VK calls used; standalone videos SHALL NOT be imported (Constraints, known limit).
- **EARS-47** _(realizes: US-5)_ — WHEN a platform administrator opens «Публикации» of a VK source, THE SYSTEM SHALL list its post and video entries as EARS-32 does, with the state «Удалён в VK» for an entry with the removed mark, and «Скрыть из ленты» / «Вернуть в ленту» of EARS-32 SHALL apply to VK entries unchanged; the note of the page SHALL read «Видео, удалённое в VK, остаётся в ленте, пока его не скрыть.» for a VK source.
- **EARS-48** _(realizes: US-6, US-7, US-10)_ — WHEN any caller reads `GET /v1/public/feed`, THE SYSTEM SHALL serve a VK post entry as a card of kind `post` with `source` `{ platform: "vk", name: "BBM Academy · VK" }`, `image` `{ url }` of the referenced VK preview or `null`, `duration`, `partner: null`, `href` the absolute `https://vk.com/wall-…` permalink and `external: true`, and a VK video entry as a card of the new kind `video` — `id`, `kind: "video"`, `title`, `publishedAt`, `image` (`{ url }` or `null`), `duration` (seconds), `source` (`{ platform: "vk", name: "BBM Academy · VK Видео" }`), `partner: null`, `href` (the absolute `https://vk.com/video-…` permalink) and `external: true` —, in the order, cursor and page of EARS-9, leaving the `news`, `column` and Telegram `post` members unchanged; an entry with the removed mark, a hidden entry and an entry of a switched-off source SHALL be absent.
- **EARS-49** _(realizes: US-6, US-7)_ — WHEN the shared feed card renders a VK `post` card, THE SYSTEM SHALL render it as EARS-34 does with the field mark «VK», the source «BBM Academy · VK», the network chip «VK» named «VK», «VK ↗» and the accessible text «, откроется в новой вкладке на площадке VK»; WHEN it renders a `video` card, THE SYSTEM SHALL show the cover with a play mark and the duration of EARS-34 or, without one, the no-image layout with the field mark «VK Видео»; the source «BBM Academy · VK Видео» with the Academy logo and the network chip «VK▶» named «VK Видео»; no kind badge; the title clamped to the lines the card holds; the date of EARS-13; and «VK Видео ↗» with the accessible text «, откроется в новой вкладке на площадке VK Видео»; WHEN a referenced preview fails to load, THE SYSTEM SHALL show that card in its no-image layout; every VK card SHALL be one keyboard-reachable link to its `href` opening in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`, and nothing SHALL play inside the Academy.
- **EARS-50** _(realizes: US-9)_ — WHILE VK is unreachable, Callback events do not arrive, a VK source is failing, the connector is not configured or a VK preview cannot be loaded, THE SYSTEM SHALL serve the feed read and the rail from the stored entries with no error state for that source, the rail showing the stored VK cards and every other card as usual.
- **EARS-51** _(realizes: US-4)_ — THE SYSTEM SHALL keep the VK service key, the Callback secret and the confirmation string in server-side configuration only, redacting the key from every outbound URL and body it logs, and SHALL write no log line, error, response, stored row or admin screen that carries any of them; of a VK publication it SHALL store only the card fields of EARS-41 and EARS-42, its keys of EARS-43, the removed mark of EARS-45 and the time of the last Callback event per source — never the raw event, `created_by`, `signer_id`, `from_id`, a video file or a copied image —, and SHALL log each VK API call with its method, HTTP status, VK error code, duration and outcome only, counting the calls per calendar month.
- **EARS-52** _(realizes: US-3, US-5, US-6)_ — WHILE the viewport is a phone or a narrow screen, THE SYSTEM SHALL keep «Источники» with a VK source and «Публикации» of a VK source usable without horizontal scrolling of the page with every action keyboard-reachable, and both pages and the Academy home carrying a VK post card and a VK Видео card SHALL pass the platform's `playwright-axe` gate.
