## Decided facts

- **Three units** [Product Lead]: Pul — the only spendable unit, never exchanged to money or to anything else; Bre — reputation; Con — awareness, only grows; 1 Con = 2 Bre; no penalties; no repeat award for the same content; one balance across the doctor storefront, the Academy and the mobile app; the admin sets each content item to open / advance / points threshold as a drop-off experiment.
- **One advance rule** [owner, 2026-11-06]: the advance is a per-event flag, the same for every doctor; a medrep or partner link records the arrival source only.
- **Everything is a parameter** [owner, 2026-11-06]: every accrual, price, cap and formula has a default and can be changed without a release; each posting records its rule version.
- **Copy model** [owner, 2026-11-05]: «бесплатно для врача» means no money and is always true; «бесплатно» never describes a Pul price; a Pul cost line only when > 0; platform points are never «баллы» in the НМО sense.
- **Bre and Con for offline learning too, Pul only online** [Product Lead, 08-06]; **a podcast outside a lesson earns nothing** [Product Lead, 08-18]; **an open webinar accrues no Pul** [Product Lead, 10-07].
- **No points ledger table exists in the code yet**; the ledger is new per ADR-0016, not an existing schema.

## Design evidence used for the defaults

- **Zero-price effect**: a move from zero to a small positive price cuts demand sharply ([Shampanier, Mazar & Ariely 2007](https://people.duke.edu/~dandan/webfiles/PapersPI/Zero%20as%20a%20Special%20Price.pdf)) — the reason lessons carry no Pul price.
- **Spend early**: the registration grant plus the profile cover the first event, as in the 2019 Starbucks Rewards change ([Starbucks 2019](https://investor.starbucks.com/news/financial-releases/news-details/2019/Starbucks-to-Enhance-Industry-Leading-Starbucks-Rewards-Loyalty-Program/default.aspx)).
- **Advance as a capped reservation, not a debt**: Marriott Points Advance was capped and later closed after speculative bookings ([One Mile at a Time 2023](https://onemileatatime.com/news/marriott-bonvoy-points-advance/)); endowed progress raises completion ([Nunes & Drèze 2006](https://econpapers.repec.org/RePEc:oup:jconrs:v:32:y:2006:i:4:p:504-512)); a visible minus contradicts «no penalties».
- **Economy tuning**: start modest and add sinks rather than cut income ([Lehdonvirta & Castronova summary](https://www.antoinebuteau.com/virtual-economies-book-summary/)); launch metrics — average Pul balance per active doctor and median time Pul sits unspent, target median ≤ two standard event prices.
- **Motivation risk**: expected rewards for participation lower intrinsic motivation ([Deci, Koestner & Ryan, PubMed 10589297](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10589297/)) — Bre and Con are presented as information about mastery, not as payment.
- **Leaderboard**: relative cohorts beat absolute tables ([Na & Han 2023](https://www.emerald.com/insight/content/doi/10.1108/intr-12-2021-0897/full/html)); competition among interns had a modest, short effect ([npj Digital Medicine 2023](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC9834206/)).
- **Anti-farming**: repeat awards for already-watched content drove farming at Khan Academy ([Khan Academy help](https://support.khanacademy.org/hc/en-us/articles/202487710-What-are-energy-points-)); a referral award after the invitee's activation, capped, as at Dropbox ([Dropbox Help](https://help.dropbox.com/accounts-billing/space-storage/how-much-free-space)).

## Compliance findings

All to be confirmed by a lawyer before launch.

- **323-FZ art. 74** forbids a medical worker to accept gifts and money, including paid entertainment, rest and travel, from pharma companies and their representatives; there is no de-minimis threshold; since September 2025 it extends to dietary-supplement and medical-device makers ([КонсультантПлюс, art. 74](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_121895/8eee23730b6e2d771575ba85beed80646a00ede9/)). An advance granted to one doctor through a company representative's link for that company's event combines all three risk signs (from a representative, individual, tied to the company's activity) — the reason for the per-event rule. Internships, travel and «perks» proposed as sinks need legal review before any catalog entry.
- **AIPM Code 2019** is stricter: no gifts (3.5), no entertainment (3.3.7), named disclosure of indirect transfers of value (ch. VII) ([AIPM Code](https://efpia.eu/media/554437/aipm-code-of-practice-2019.pdf)). The cleanest model is ACCME Standard 4: sponsor money may lower the cost of education «for all learners», never pay individual costs ([ACCME Standards 2022](https://luminishealth.cloud-cme.com/assets/luminishealth/pdf/ACCME_STANDARDS_FOR_INTEGRITY_AND_INDEPENDENCE_IN_ACCREDITED_CONTINUING_EDUCATION_2022.pdf)).
- **НМО**: events organised by manufacturers are not assessed for НМО, and an accredited event must contain no form of advertising — so НМО-accredited events are always open and sponsor content stays outside НМО. The idea of extra Pul for watching a partner video, and «more advertising — more Pul», pay value for attention to promotion and are excluded at launch.
- **152-FZ**: public display of name, specialty and activity is dissemination and needs a separate art. 10.1 consent with field choice and immediate withdrawal ([КонсультантПлюс, art. 10.1](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_61801/591acc70f577873c1ee54765eda110b7a0271eaf/)); since 1 September 2025 any consent is a separate document, not a clause of the user agreement ([Гарант](https://www.garant.ru/article/1862510/)) — this rules out the 08-18 proposal to put partner-data consent into the user agreement. Named sponsor reports need their own consent; aggregates are the default. Whether a service may depend on a consent to third-party transfer is a question for the lawyer; ADR-0016 closes the account and freezes the balance on such a refusal without deleting postings.
