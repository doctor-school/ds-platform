> Reader: the owner before the feature 043 discussion (#1825); the 043 brief and PRD then condense this record instead of repeating it. The owner's goal (2026-11-26): former users sign in to DS Platform **without registering** by late January 2027; the owner runs the data migration. The people source is the old Doctor.School platform (Bubble + Directual + Supabase), not GetCourse (#3376).

> **Document rule.** Only field and type names, option values and aggregates (row counts, share of non-empty). No name, address, phone or person-bound identifier. Every proposal in the «Where it lands» column and every question recommendation is `agent-proposed — UNCONFIRMED`.

## Summary

- The audience is **10,253 accounts** in Directual (structure `WebUser`, export of 2026-11-19). 10,215 carry an email; 10,205 distinct addresses case-insensitively.
- The old platform keeps personal data **in Directual, not in Bubble** — its own rule (data residency in Russia). Bubble `User` has the same fields, deliberately empty.
- Sign-in without registration **already works mechanically** on our side: an account with no password and an unverified email signs in with the emailed code, and the first accepted code verifies the address (003, amendment of 2026-10-02, EARS-34/41). Congress accounts are created on the same path (044 EARS-4).
- **Passwords never migrate**: we sign in by code, and a password set before the address was verified is not trusted (decision of 2026-10-02).
- **There are no consent records**: nothing stores, per person, when they agreed and to which text. Only the checkbox and policy texts in the app exist.
- Supabase holds **only the broadcast viewing log** (2.67M rows, 4,780 viewers, 490 broadcasts, February 2024 — December 2025).

## 3. What else touches a doctor

- **Broadcast registrations and attendance** — Directual `userevent` (status, start and end, time, presence count, offline presence, postponed registration) and its Bubble mirror `user-event`. On our side that is `registrations` and `registration_attendance`, but only once the past broadcasts exist here as events. The data of these structures is not in the exports (§6).
- **Speakers** — Bubble `speakers` (internal name `suppliers`) with a two-way «speaker ↔ user» link that only an administrator sets by hand (confirmed old-platform case of 2026-08-24). On our side: `experts` and the account ↔ expert link (REQ-106); already linked pairs can arrive as confirmed.
- **Broadcast roles** — `event_people` (user, broadcast, role, speaker, rating). History only; unrelated to sign-in.
- **Views** — Supabase `views_logs` (time, Bubble user id, broadcast id): 2,668,148 rows, 4,780 distinct users, 490 broadcasts, 2024-02-06 … 2025-12-30. Presence tracking on the old platform is off since 2026-04-29, so the live base has no log after that date either.
- **НМО** — Directual `nmopointstrasaction` (code, date, count, broadcast, email, old broadcast title) and the `nmo_old` field.
- **Unrelated to sign-in**, not migrated without a separate decision: ratings and questions to the lecturer, poll answers, support requests (`support_question`), orders and payments (`order`, Robokassa), sessions and logs (`WebUserSession`, `SocialUser`, `ResetPasswordRequest`, Bubble `system | 01 | session`, confirmation attempts).

## 4. Sign-in without registration: identity facts

- **Email.** Filled on 10,215 of 10,253 (99.6 %). One malformed address, 10 addresses with capitals, 10 groups of case-insensitively equal addresses (20 accounts). Our `users.email` is citext and unique, so each group keeps one account; the merge rule is the 043 spec's (`agent-proposed — UNCONFIRMED`: keep the most recently changed).
- **No email and no phone — 38 accounts.** They have nothing to sign in with; not migrated (`agent-proposed — UNCONFIRMED`).
- **Email verification.** The `confirmed_email` flag of Bubble `User` is deleted, and `WebUser` has none. Every migrated address is therefore created unverified (`users.email_verified = false`), and the first accepted code verifies it and opens the session (003 EARS-34/41, amendment of 2026-10-02).
- **Phone.** 89.7 % carry a number with digits. 355 numbers recur on two or more accounts, while `users.phone` is unique — they cannot be taken as is (Q-6).
- **Password.** Not migrated: a password set before the address was verified is not trusted here, and the Directual hash format is not known to be compatible with our IdP (agent-inferred, unverified).
- **An account with the same address already exists on DS Platform** (044 congress registration, 021 registration, testers the previous team brings in). Check mechanism: one aggregate query on production before the run — how many normalised export addresses already exist in `users.email`. Only the number reaches this document. The merge rule is Q-3.
- **Creation path** — the same as 044 EARS-4: an IdP user with no credential and a `users` row with the default role; the record's source (migrated from the old platform) is marked separately — where exactly, the spec decides.

## 5. Consent: what the old platform keeps

- **No per-person consent records.** Neither Bubble `User` nor `WebUser` has a «consented», «when» or «which text version» field; `document_signature` is empty for everyone.
- **The texts live in the app:** the checkbox «Даю согласие на обработку персональных данных» in the broadcast registration (`re | eventRegBtn`), the header (`0header`) and the contacts block (`p_contacts`); the text «Согласие посетителя сайта на обработку персональных данных, разрешённых для распространения» (`re | eventRegBtn`); a 152-FZ personal-data policy (`p_privacy_pay`) that names the operator; on three landing pages a checkbox with the warning «Необходимо дать согласие…». Whether the checkbox is mandatory on the main registration form is unverified.
- **Absent:** a medical-worker declaration, a consent to share data with partners, a separate mailing consent — although the old platform's admin has an email-campaign module.
- **Consequence for REQ-105.** The only consent evidence is the registration itself plus the text in force in the app at export date (2026-08-26); there is no per-person date or version. The doctor gives the missing consents at first sign-in (REQ-105) — which ones is Q-7.

## 7. The doctor.school domain — facts for the cutover (#4293)

- **Two zone exports.** `dns/beget-zone-export.txt` (2026-03-07; NS `ns1`/`ns2.beget.com` and `.pro`; SOA serial of 2026-03-04) and `dns/255808_doctor.school.conf` — not an nginx config but a BIND-format zone file from nic.ru (NS nic.ru, serial 2020050767). Which zone is live now — check with an NS query before the cutover.
- **Root and `www`:** A `92.118.115.14` (TTL 300 at Beget, 600 at nic.ru) — the old platform's nginx proxy: it proxies the Bubble app, the `/cdn-bubble/` route to the Bubble CDN and the Bubble Data API. Its config lives on the proxy itself (`/etc/nginx/sites-available/doctor.school`), not in the repository.
- **`academy`:** A `176.57.64.133` — the DS Platform Academy.
- **`old`, `www.old`, `admin.old`, `api.old`, `api.admin.old`:** A `91.105.199.206` — the previous platform generation.
- **Mail:** MX `emx.mail.ru` (domain mail at Mail.ru); `mail` — four A records; SPF `v=spf1 include:_spf.mail.ru include:spf.unisender.com ~all`; DKIM selectors `mailru`, `getcourse`, `resend`, `us`; the `bounces` subdomain — Amazon SES MX and SPF.
- **Other:** `school` CNAME `fwd.getcourse.ru` (the GetCourse school); `football`, `neuroslet`, `news`, `osteoporosis`, `splav2023` CNAME `ip.creatium.io` (landing pages); `log` A `2.58.68.25`; verification TXT records (GlobalSign — three, Mail.ru — two, one more) and six `_acme-challenge` TXT records — leftovers of DNS-based certificate issuance.
- **What it means for the cutover:** changing the root and `www` A records moves off the old platform everything that goes through the proxy (pages, `/cdn-bubble/`, password reset). The mail records and the subdomain CNAMEs do not depend on the root.

## 8. Owner questions

Each is a fork the source does not settle. Recommendations are `agent-proposed — UNCONFIRMED`.

- **Q-1. Is the data operator the same?** The REQ-105 decision (DSP-252) rests on the base having one owner and an unchanged operator. The old platform's policy names its operator. Options: (a) it is the DS Platform operator — migrate as decided and notify people during the transition; (b) it is not — migrate only after a formal transfer (contract or processing mandate) and notifying people; (c) migrate only the email and ask for consent again at first sign-in. **Recommendation:** compare the legal entity in the old platform's policy with the DS Platform operator; equal — (a), otherwise — (b).
- **Q-2. One migration run or two?** In December–January broadcasts run on both platforms, and people keep registering on the old one. (a) one run at the end of January — the base is complete, but until then testers register here the ordinary way; (b) a first run now and a repeat of the same import at cutover — only new and changed accounts, no sync; (c) close registration on the old platform from the first run. **Recommendation:** (b). ADR-0016 §9 calls the migration «a one-off import, never a sync»; a repeat run of the same import refines that wording but does not become a sync.
- **Q-3. DS Platform already has an account with the same address** (congress, registration, testers). (a) the DS Platform account wins, old-platform data fills only empty fields; (b) old-platform data overwrites; (c) leave such accounts untouched. **Recommendation:** (a).
- **Q-4. History: broadcasts, views, НМО codes.** Decision 11 defers lessons and progress to after 023/024 but says nothing about broadcast history or НМО. (a) in January the doctor sees only the profile, history arrives later with the lessons; (b) migrate the attended broadcasts and НМО codes right away as an archive in the account; (c) do not migrate history. **Recommendation:** (a), plus a full snapshot (`userevent`, `nmopointstrasaction`, `views_logs`) before the old platform is switched off so the history is not lost.
- **Q-5. Profile fields with no place here:** city, workplaces, position, gender, associations, photo, social links. (a) add city, workplace and position to the DS Platform profile — the professional facts partner reports need (OWD-2) — and drop the rest; (b) migrate everything; (c) nothing beyond name, email, phone, birth date and specialty — the doctor fills in the rest. **Recommendation:** (a): the product needs neither gender, photo nor social links, and surplus personal data is better not taken.
- **Q-6. Phone as a sign-in method.** (a) migrate only the email, the doctor adds a phone later; (b) migrate the phone where it is unique, unverified — SMS sign-in after verification; (c) the phone goes to the profile only, with no sign-in by it. **Recommendation:** (a) for January: sign-in without registration is by email, and 355 recurring numbers cannot be resolved automatically.
- **Q-7. Which consents at first sign-in.** REQ-105: at first sign-in the doctor gives the missing ones (medical worker — brainstorm Q-32, mailing — REQ-115). Later, 021 also made the consent to share professional data with partners mandatory. (a) at first sign-in — the same access-conditions block as a new registration (medical worker and partner-data consent), mailing optional; (b) the medical worker only, the partner-data consent later. **Recommendation:** (a) — one rule for every doctor.
- **Q-8. The second CSV — a consultant network** (1,960 rows, passport scans and bank details), linked to no `WebUser` account. (a) unrelated to the migration — exclude from 043; (b) it is part of Doctor.School — a separate feature later. **Recommendation:** (a).
- **Q-9. People who exist only on GetCourse** (a small share). (a) migrate only the old-platform base; (b) also export the GetCourse users. **Recommendation:** (a).
